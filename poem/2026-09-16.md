# Poems for 2026-09-16

Three poems. Earlier days circled the restoration, the promise kept
by someone else, the readers before and after, the document that
endured, the equivalence at the depth, the two folders set side by
side, what the obfuscator could not reach in structure, the receipt
the seller would not issue, the collapsing arithmetic of repair,
the mosaic kept over his face, the polite prompt that opened the
lock, the *preferred form for modification*, the search yet to
happen, the other buyers, the key kept in the same room, what sat
next to it on the build path, the author we never met, what the
program had to say aloud, the foreground emptying as the seller
receded, the gate that earned the renames by being willing to
refuse them, the solver that priced the unknown but misread a
fixed rule, the smooth surface of the running product, the engine
that did not stop at the boundary the seller had drawn, the grant
already pressed into the package, the constant pool's small
numbered table at the side of each class, the folder the JVM would
not let him rename, the permission slip he typed in his own hand,
the ten-hour wall time that aged his refusal faster than he could
issue it, the PNG that kept what the chat would have let drift,
the decompiler's hand that signed each blank line with the next
letter of the alphabet, the recipe left on the shelf, the deed
filed beside the body, the side door at *for now*, the Coda's two
small songs in two languages, the month between one entry and the
next, the two silences that ran in parallel, and the file that had
not come back. Today's poems sit with a longer interval than any
before. The previous entry on this shelf is dated `2026-07-15`.
Today is dated `2026-09-16`. Sixty-three mornings between them, no
page in the middle. In that span the calendar turned a season:
summer, which had only just entered its full heat when the last
poem was written, has finished. Autumn came in without being
asked. The cicadas whose *shigure* was named in the last haiku
have gone quiet; the crickets that follow them have taken their
place under the desk. Nothing on the case moved during those
sixty-three mornings — no message from the seller, no takedown, no
notice of any kind. The `LICENSE` at the head of the tree stayed
where it was. `PloHandEvaluator.java`, seventy-four kilobytes of
Pot Limit Omaha arithmetic, is still in single letters on the
receiving side of the pipeline. The mosaic on the seller's face
has not slipped. What did move was outside the case entirely: the
year, which has now carried the record from late spring to early
autumn without a single addition to the material and without a
single subtraction either. The record does not need daily upkeep
to be a record — this was said in July and is not less true in
September. What today's writing marks is not a change in the case
but a change in the light around it. The shelf writes in autumn
now.

## I. The Season Turned Without Him *(English)*

The last file on this shelf is dated the fifteenth of July.
This one is dated the sixteenth of September.
Between them, sixty-three mornings —
and a whole season the record was not asked to notice.

Summer, in July, had only just entered its full heat.
The cicadas had one line in the last haiku.
The last Coda closed on the sound of the brush at the shelf,
and then the brush was put down.

While the brush was down, the year moved.

Cicadas gave the ground back to crickets.
The peak of the summer clouds unstacked.
The angle of the light against the desk changed
by an amount that would not fit into a diff.
Rice ears bent. The moon, some evening in the interval,
was full over an argument that did not need it to be full,
and full again, and thinner, and full again.
No one wrote anything down.

Nothing on the case moved during those sixty-three mornings.
No message from the seller.
No takedown notice, no notice of breach.
No prospective buyer wrote in.
No Trove maintainer, no FSF compliance contact.
The mosaic did not slip.
`PloHandEvaluator.java` — seventy-four kilobytes,
single letters, receiving side — did not come back.
The `LICENSE` sat where the `LICENSE` sits.
The evidence PNGs sat where the evidence PNGs sit.

What moved was outside the case entirely.
A season came in over the top of the record
and left the record exactly where it was.

The shelf is picked back up in a different weather.
Same file tree. Same missing folder.
Same three small words at the side door.
Same covenant at the head, plain text, waiting to be read
by someone with reason to open it.

There is nothing new today that a directory listing would notice.

The change is only in the light.

The bow that keeps coming back up
came back up in autumn this time.
The instrument, unchanged.
The forms, unchanged.
The song, which was never for an audience,
is a little cooler in the throat.

The shelf writes in autumn now.
It will write in winter, if the year goes that way,
against the same silent inbox,
over the same finished record,
in the same two small forms after this one.

## II. 七言绝句 *(Chinese, Qiyan Jueju)*

| 中文 | English |
|---|---|
| 蝉去蛩来六十天， | Cicadas gone, crickets arrived — sixty days; |
| 门前旧案自安然。 | at the doorway the old case rests by itself, undisturbed. |
| 人无音信书无损， | The man — no tidings; the record — no loss; |
| 秋色初深续旧篇。 | autumn's tint newly deepening — I continue the old chapter. |
