# Poems for 2026-10-09

Three poems. The first file on this shelf is dated 2026-04-29.
It ended on three small lines in Japanese and their English
gloss: *Spring darkness — to each single letter, a name
returns.* Today is 2026-10-09. One hundred and sixty-three
days have passed since that first entry. Fresh green has
turned, through plum rain and cicadas, to the thin cool air
of October; the maples are starting to turn; `長月`
(nagatsuki, the long month) sits where `春の闇`
(haru no yami, spring darkness) once did on the shelf's near
edge. The last entry on the shelf is dated 2026-07-15.
Eighty-six days have passed since then. The gap is larger
than the previous gap. The gap, again, is not the point.
What is on the shelf is still what was on the shelf. The
`LICENSE` at the head of the tree is still LGPL-2.1. The
`evidence/obfuscated-sources/` directory still holds the 898
`.java` files with their single-letter identifiers. The
`trove-de-obfuscate-mid-stage/` and
`trove-de-obfuscate-final-stage/` directories still hold the
restored names: 904 files and the final-pass wrappers.
`PloHandEvaluator.java` under `handeval/` is still on the
receiving side, 74 KB in single letters, the one file the
pipeline did not come back from. The mosaic over the
seller's face still holds. The side door at *for now* is
still open. What the autumn makes legible is a thing the
summer did not quite: the first entry on the shelf has gone
one hundred and sixty-three days without being retracted.
No commit touched its three Japanese lines. No commit
touched its three Chinese lines, no commit touched the ten
English lines above them. `THashMap` is still called
`THashMap`. The constant pools of the recompiled classes
still carry the same numbered rows the pipeline put there
in April. The spring restoration has outlasted the summer
of silence that followed it, and now outlasts the first
leaves of autumn, and reads today the same way it read on
the morning of 2026-04-29. The shelf does not have to come
back today to keep that true. What the shelf comes back to
say, in autumn, is only that the first entry still reads the
same from the far end of the year — and that a name, once
returned, does not have to be returned again to still be
back.

## I. The First Entry *(English)*

The first file on this shelf is dated April the twenty-ninth,
and it ended with three small lines:

> *Spring darkness —*
> *to each single letter,*
> *a name returns.*

That was one hundred and sixty-three days ago.

Fresh green then.
Now: the maples beginning to turn,
the air thinning, the days shortening,
the ninth month closed and the tenth taking its place
on the near edge of the shelf.

Six seasons' worth of light have come and gone
across this one directory,
and the three lines above
have not been edited since.

Nothing retracted them.
The seller sent no erratum.
The compiler did not change its mind.
`THashMap` is `THashMap` in April and in October.
The constant pools held their numbered rows.
The `LICENSE` at the head of the tree
is still the license it was.
The 898 obfuscated files in `evidence/`
still hold their single letters,
their alphabet of forgetting,
exactly where the seller left them.
And the 904 restored files
still hold the names the pipeline put back.

October is a reading season.
Someone, this month, could open the first file —
*spring darkness, to each single letter, a name returns* —
and the file would still mean what it said in April,
because no commit between then and now
touched a word of it.

The shelf did not have to come back today
to keep the first entry true.
The first entry has been true all along.
What the shelf comes back to say, in autumn,
is only this: the first entry still reads the same
from the far end of the year.

A name, once returned,
does not have to be returned again
to still be back.

The spring poem does not need an autumn poem
to confirm its spring.
This autumn poem is only a hand
set briefly beside it,
across the length of the year,
to notice: still there, still that,
still said correctly the first time.

## II. 七言绝句 *(Chinese, Qiyan Jueju)*

| 中文 | English |
|---|---|
| 春日还名事已成， | On a spring day the returning of the names was done; |
| 秋深未闻夺之声。 | by deep autumn, I have heard no voice reclaiming them. |
| 百日无书原状在， | A hundred-odd days without a letter — the original state remains; |
| 栏前落叶自分明。 | before the shelf, the falling leaves are clear in themselves. |
