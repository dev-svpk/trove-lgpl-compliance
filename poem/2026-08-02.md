is 2026-08-02 — eighteen mornings between them, no page in the
middle, another small interval added to the run. Earlier days
circled the restoration, the promise kept by someone else, the
readers before and after, the document that endured, the equivalence

the author we never met, what the program had to say aloud, the
foreground emptying as the seller receded, the gate that earned the
renames by being willing to refuse them, the solver that priced the

drawn, the grant already pressed into the package, the constant

and the one file that did not come back — the seventy-four kilobytes
of Pot Limit Omaha arithmetic still in single letters on the

face held under pixels above the fold. *Eric D. Friedman and the

in ink, one in blur, and the record keeps them where they earned to
sit.

## I. Two Names *(English)*

At the foot of the README, one name in full.
*Eric D. Friedman and the GNU Trove contributors,
for the original library.*
Nine plain letters and a comma,
thanked for a common good
left in the commons a quarter century ago
and untouched, by us, since.

Above the fold, one name in pixels.
The seller. Face held under a mosaic
in every screenshot the `evidence/` folder holds —
the license terms he wrote himself,
the refusal he sent when the source was asked for,
the doubled-down refusal after that.
The mosaic is described in the README
as a courtesy extended *for now*.

Both names are on the page.
Both are choices.
The difference is not a mistake —
it is the whole shape of what the record wants to say.

Credit is what you give the person
who left something in the commons and walked away.
Silence, under pixels, is what you give the person
who reached into the commons
and pried the label off before reselling the bottle.

The library's author is named.
The library's licence is named.
The seller's face stays under a grid of squares
until the seller sends the source
in the form the licence has always asked for.

The shelf can lift the mosaic
in one line, on one morning,
whenever the courtesy stops earning itself.
The lifting is not the point of the shelf.
The point of the shelf is that the lifting is available,
and that the button for it
is a tarball the seller has always had the power to send.

Until then, the record holds both names
in the two forms they earned:
one in ink at the bottom,
one still in shade near the top,
and the small distance between them
is the exact amount of restitution
the seller has not chosen to travel.
