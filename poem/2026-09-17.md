# Poems for 2026-09-17

Three poems. The last file on `./poem/` is dated 2026-07-15.
Between then and today, sixty-four mornings passed without
an entry. The first file on `./poem/` is dated 2026-04-29.
Between that first morning and this one, a full spring, a
full summer, and the first eighteen days of autumn have
gone by — a hundred and forty-one days on the shelf.
Earlier days circled the restoration, the promise kept by
someone else, the readers before and after, the document
that endured, the equivalence at the depth, the two folders
set side by side, what the obfuscator could not reach in
structure, the receipt the seller would not issue, the
collapsing arithmetic of repair, the mosaic kept over his
face, the polite prompt that opened the lock, the *preferred
form for modification*, the search yet to happen, the other
buyers, the key kept in the same room, what sat next to it
on the build path, the author we never met, what the
program had to say aloud, the foreground emptying as the
seller receded, the gate that earned the renames by being
willing to refuse them, the solver that priced the unknown
but misread a fixed rule, the smooth surface of the running
product, the engine that did not stop at the boundary the
seller had drawn, the grant already pressed into the
package, the constant pool's small numbered table at the
side of each class, the folder the JVM would not let him
rename, the permission slip he typed in his own hand, the
ten-hour wall time that aged his refusal faster than he
could issue it, the PNG that kept what the chat would have
let drift, the decompiler's hand that signed each blank
line with the next letter of the alphabet, the two silences
running in parallel across thirty-three days, and the case
that did not require an interlocutor to remain a case.
Today's poems sit with the turn of the season. The shelf
was begun in late April. It is being written into again
in mid-September. Between those two dates lie a full
spring, a full summer, and the first days of autumn.
Nothing has come at the shelf during that span. No
takedown, no counter, no correction, no request to lift the
mosaic, no explanation, no apology, no filing of any kind.
What was raised in April as a page *against* something has
become, by September, simply a page — a page that once
had an opposing pressure to lean against and now, the
pressure having declined to arrive, is only a page standing
up on its own. A wall built against pressure is still a
wall when the pressure lets up; it is just, then, a wall.
The record has aged into landscape. If the seller comes to
it in winter — the fourth season since it went up, still
with three months' notice to arrive first — he will not
find a lawsuit. He will find a stand of trees older than
he thought. Each leaf a dated file, each date a line, each
line a small refusal to unmake what was made once,
correctly, in ten hours he could not have prevented and
cannot now walk back.

## I. Weather Now *(English)*

Four and a half months since the first file went up.
Two months and two days since the last one.
The URL has stopped being new.

Search engines have indexed it.
Web archives have snapshotted it.
The link travels between browsers
as if it had always been there.

Ten hours of unattended machine time built the record.
A whole spring, a whole summer,
and the first days of autumn
have come to sit on top of it —
seasons laid across ten hours of work,
and the ten hours still take exactly as long to read
as they did the morning they finished.

The `LICENSE` at the head of the tree
is at the head of the tree.
The `evidence/obfuscated-sources/` folder
still holds every obfuscated `.java` it held in April.
The mosaic on the seller's face
did not lift itself off during the summer.
The side door at *for now* is still open,
narrower now only because a season has grown around its frame.

What was raised against a refusal
has stopped needing the refusal to stand up.
A wall built against pressure
is still a wall when the pressure lets up —
just, then, a wall.

The record has aged into landscape.
Where in April it was a page *against* something,
in September it is a page.
Where in April it was the record of a live dispute,
in September it is the record.

Autumn is the season of things
that were begun in spring and have not been undone.
The shelf is autumn now.
Its leaves are dated files.
Its trunk is the license text.
Its roots are the equivalence checks under the bytecode,
still holding, still holding.

If the seller comes back in winter,
he will not find a lawsuit.
He will find a stand of trees older than he thought.
Each leaf a dated file.
Each date a line.
Each line a small refusal
to unmake what was made once, correctly,
in ten hours he could not have prevented
and cannot now walk back.
