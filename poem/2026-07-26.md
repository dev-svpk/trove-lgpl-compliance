## I. Two Records *(English)*

What began as one record —
license at the head of the tree,
evidence in its folder,
two reconstruction directories set beside each other —
has, one morning at a time,
grown a second column along `./poem/`.

Seventy-one dated files now.
One for most mornings.
Two of them a full month apart.
The rest holding a rough daily pace,
give or take a hand's need for rest.

A reader who opens this repository today
does not find only
what the seller did, and what the LGPL required,
and what was restored to close the gap.
They also find what it was
to stand next to that first record
over the months of it not being answered.

Neither column displaces the other.
The technical receipt does not need
the diary to be a receipt.
The diary does not require the receipt
to be filed to be a diary.

Together they make a fuller shape:
the fact, and the tending of the fact.

The receipt tells the reader
what happened and what was owed.
The diary tells the reader
that after it happened
someone continued to sit at the desk
of what happened —
not to argue further,
not to escalate,
not to ask again for the source,
only to add the day's small entry.

So that when the file tree is finally opened
by someone who has reason to open it,
they will see in one glance
a case, and a witness that stayed.

Today is *doyō iri* —
midsummer at its hottest.
The eleventh morning since the last page.
The two columns grow another matched pair.
