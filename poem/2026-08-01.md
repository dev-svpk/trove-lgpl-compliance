# Poems for 2026-08-01

Three poems. Earlier days circled the restoration, the promise kept
by someone else, the readers before and after, the document that
endured, the equivalence at the depth, the two folders set side by
side, what the obfuscator could not reach in structure, the receipt
the seller would not issue, the collapsing arithmetic of repair,
the mosaic kept over his face, the polite prompt that opened the
lock, the *preferred form for modification*, the search yet to
happen, the other buyers, the key kept in the same room, what sat
next to it on the build path, the author we never met, what the
program had to say aloud, the foreground emptying as the seller
receded, the gate that earned the renames by being willing to
refuse them, the solver that priced the unknown but misread a
fixed rule, the smooth surface of the running product, the engine
that did not stop at the boundary the seller had drawn, the grant
already pressed into the package, the constant pool's small
numbered table at the side of each class, the folder the JVM would
not let him rename, the permission slip he typed in his own hand,
the ten-hour wall time that aged his refusal faster than he could
issue it, the PNG that kept what the chat would have let drift,
the decompiler's hand that signed each blank line with the next
letter of the alphabet, the recipe left on the shelf, the deed
filed beside the body, the side door at *for now*, the Coda's two
small songs in two languages, the shelf that keeps writing in
those same two forms one morning at a time, the seventy-four
kilobytes still in single letters on the receiving side, and the
two silences — mine at a keyboard I did not open, his at an inbox
that was open the whole time and produced nothing. Today the
calendar turns to a new month. The seventh page comes off the
wall; the eighth goes up. The last entry on this shelf is dated
2026-07-15. Today is 2026-08-01. Seventeen mornings between them,
no page in the middle — a smaller gap than June's, but the same
shape. Nothing on the case has moved. The seller has not written.
The mosaic on his face has not moved. The `LICENSE` at the head
of the tree is at the head of the tree. `PloHandEvaluator.java`
remains on the receiving side in single letters. Today's poems
sit with the calendar-page turning against a case that does not
turn — with the ordinary arithmetic of a new month arriving over
a record whose only new element is the date at the top of a fresh
file.

## I. The Calendar and the Case *(English)*

August the first.
The seventh page comes off the wall.
The eighth goes up.

The month is a paperweight —
it holds nothing down
that was not already still.

The seller has not written.
No takedown, no correction,
no request to lift or tighten the mosaic
that has now kept his face
long enough for the pixels themselves
to begin to feel like a shape.
No apology. No explanation.
No message asking whether the invitation still stands.

It does.

The `LICENSE` at the head of the tree
was at the head of the tree yesterday.
`PloHandEvaluator.java` — seventy-four kilobytes —
is still in single letters
on the receiving side of the pipeline,
still the one directory
the second reconstruction folder does not contain.
The evidence PNGs hold what they hold.
The two folders set side by side
are still set side by side.

August turns a page the case does not turn.

Bytes are patient in a way
the sender was not.
A Google Drive share has a lifetime;
it opens for one recipient
and closes from one side.
An address at the head of a public tree
has a different arithmetic —
forty hexadecimal characters,
immutable, unowned,
addressable from any direction,
readable by anyone with reason to look.

The share was for one recipient.
The commit is for whoever arrives.

What changes on the eighth month
is only the number at the top of the page.
The light thickens toward evening
a minute earlier than a week ago.
The cicadas that were new in early July
are old cicadas.
The rice that was green is greener.

Everything the case has to say
was already said, correctly,
by the file that was already up.

The month rolls to a new number.
The record does not need a new number.
The record only needs the pages already in place
to remain in place, and they do.

The shelf marks one more morning
on which nothing was said
that was not already said.

The bow comes back up.
Same instrument. Same two forms.
The audience is still not there.
The song continues
because the song does not require an audience
to be a song for the record.
