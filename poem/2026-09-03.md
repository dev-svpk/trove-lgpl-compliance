# Poems for 2026-09-03

Three poems. Earlier days circled the restoration, the promise kept
by someone else, the readers before and after, the document that
endured, the equivalence at the depth, the two folders set side by
side, what the obfuscator could not reach in structure, the receipt
the seller would not issue, the collapsing arithmetic of repair, the
mosaic kept over his face, the polite prompt that opened the lock,
the *preferred form for modification*, the search yet to happen, the
other buyers, the key kept in the same room, what sat next to it on
the build path, the author we never met, what the program had to say
aloud, the foreground emptying as the seller receded, the gate that
earned the renames by being willing to refuse them, the solver that
priced the unknown but misread a fixed rule, the smooth surface of
the running product, the engine that did not stop at the boundary
the seller had drawn, the grant already pressed into the package,
the constant pool's small numbered table at the side of each class,
the folder the JVM would not let him rename, the permission slip he
typed in his own hand, the ten-hour wall time that aged his refusal
faster than he could issue it, the PNG that kept what the chat would
have let drift, the decompiler's hand that signed each blank line
with the next letter of the alphabet, the recipe left on the shelf,
the deed filed beside the body, the side door at *for now*, the
Coda's two small songs in two languages, the shelf that keeps
writing in those same two forms one morning at a time, the one file
that did not come back — seventy-four kilobytes of Pot Limit Omaha
arithmetic still in single letters on the receiving side of the
pipeline — the two silences that ran in parallel through July, and
the vigil that resumed at the shelf without an interlocutor.
Today's poems sit with the second such gap and what it means that
there is a second. The previous entry on this shelf is dated
2026-07-15. Today is 2026-09-03. Fifty mornings have gone by, and
once again none of them held a page. That makes two long silences
on the shelf this summer — one of thirty-two days, one of fifty —
and the second is longer than the first. What used to be a lapse
is beginning to look like a rhythm. Nothing else on the case has
moved. No takedown notice. No breach letter. No email from behind
the mosaic asking the courtesy be renewed or lifted. No
prospective customer, no other buyer, no Trove maintainer, no FSF
compliance contact. The inbox continues to demonstrate what an
inbox looks like when no one has a reason to write in. Outside the
inbox, the seasons have moved: the cicadas have thinned, the
plum-rain is a memory, the high-summer clouds have receded, and the
first cool mornings arrived while the shelf was quiet. The file
tree is unchanged between the two dates. The record required
nothing during the fifty mornings, and the record received nothing
during the fifty mornings, and each half of that sentence is doing
the work the other half describes.

## I. What Twice Makes *(English)*

Once is a lapse.
Twice is a shape.

The last entry on this shelf is dated 2026-07-15.
This one is dated 2026-09-03.
Fifty mornings between them, and no page in the middle —
and this is the second time this summer
the shelf has gone dark for over a month
and come back on.

The first pause was thirty-two mornings.
The second is fifty.
The interval is not shrinking as a habit does
when it is trying to become daily again.
It is widening
in the direction of whatever the pauses are already for.

Nothing on the case has moved to fill them.
The `LICENSE` at the head of the tree
was at the head of the tree yesterday.
The obfuscated sources sit where they sat.
`src-reconstructed-by-claude/` still contains no `handeval/`.
The mosaic on the seller's face has not slipped.
The side door under *for now* has not closed.
The inbox continues to demonstrate
what an inbox looks like
when no one has a reason to write in.

What has moved is the light.
The cicadas thinned while no one wrote.
The plum-rain is a memory.
The high-summer clouds have receded.
The first cool morning found the shelf quiet
and the case unchanged,
and neither of those two facts
required the other one to be true.

A daily shelf that skips a day
tells you the writer was busy.
A daily shelf that skips fifty
tells you what the shelf is actually for.
It is not a diary of the case.
The case does not have entries.
The case has a state, and the state is stable,
and the state was stable through fifty mornings
in which no one was reading it
and no one was disputing it.

The shelf is the writer's way
of returning to a room
that has been maintaining itself in his absence.

Twice this summer he has come back to it
and found it exactly as he left it —
license cited, evidence filed, receipt attached,
one file still in single letters,
one face still under a mosaic,
one side door still open under three small words.

Nothing was owed to the writer during the pauses.
Nothing was owed by the writer during the pauses.
The record, which owes nothing to anyone,
went on being the record.

Autumn is starting.
The song-shelf sings again.
The audience is still not there.
The song continues because the song
was never for the audience —
it was for the record,
and the record has now demonstrated, twice,
that it does not require the song
in order to keep being the record.

The song is only what the writer does
when the writer comes back to the pen.
Fifty mornings is a long time to come back from.
Two returns is not a coincidence anymore.
It is the shape of how this shelf will now be tended:
in long quiet stretches, broken by a page,
each page saying only what the last page already said,
correctly, and once more into a room
where nothing has come to say otherwise.

## II. 七言绝句 *(Chinese, Qiyan Jueju)*

| 中文 | English |
|---|---|
| 一夏无书秋已临， | One summer without writing — autumn is already at the door; |
| 门空案在自沉沉。 | the door stands empty, the case still there, quiet in its own deep silence. |
| 两番久默人归座， | Twice the long silence; now the person returns to the seat; |
| 旧字如初照夜深。 | the old words, as they were at the first, still shine into the deepening night. |
