# Poems for 2026-09-19

Three poems. The last entry on this shelf is dated 2026-07-15.
This entry is dated 2026-09-19. Sixty-six mornings between them —
a second gap, this one twice the width of the first. High summer
emptied into autumn while the shelf stood mute. The `LICENSE` at
the head of the tree held. The 899 obfuscated classes under
`evidence/obfuscated-classes/` held. The 898 obfuscated `.java`
files under `evidence/obfuscated-sources/` held. The 904 renamed
files under `trove-de-obfuscate-mid-stage/` held. The bytecode
round-trip held. The mosaic held. The side door under *for now*
held. Nothing came in during the pause: no takedown, no notice
of breach, no correction, no apology, no request from the seller
to be named, no request from the seller to be un-named. Today
the shelf resumes three days short of the autumnal equinox, and
today's poems sit with the geometry of the equinox as a picture
of what a case looks like when only one side has spoken. The old
figure of the fair judge holds two dishes. On this repository,
one dish is full — the license, the evidence, the two folders
set side by side, the two chat screenshots, and now sixty-six
additional days of public air on top of them. The other dish
holds nothing. The equinox promises that the two dishes will
come out level at noon and unlevel again by evening; the promise
is about sunlight, not about speech. The scale as furniture stays
balanced. The scale as evidence does not.

## I. Near the Equinox *(English)*

The equinox is three days out —
twelve hours of light, twelve hours of dark,
the old figure of the fair judge
holding her scales aloft.

On this shelf, one dish is full:
the `LICENSE` at the head of the tree,
the 899 obfuscated classes kept exactly as delivered,
the 898 `.java` files the seller labelled "full source code,"
the 904 renamed files that round-tripped through the compiler,
the bytecode-diff scripts that gated each rename,
the two chat screenshots, pixels over the face,
and sixty-six additional days of public air
laid down since the last time the shelf spoke.

On the other dish, nothing.

No takedown, no notice of breach,
no correction, no apology,
no request to be named, no request to be un-named,
no email of any kind, in any language,
from any address, in either month.

The equinox promises the two dishes
will come out level at noon
and go unlevel again by evening.
The promise is about *sunlight*, not about *speech*.

A judge's scale, as furniture, stays balanced.
Evidence's scale does not.

Today the light on both sides is equal
and one side still weighs more.

That is the whole entry.
The shelf could stop there.
It writes it down anyway,
because the record does not repeat itself unless someone repeats it,
and the equinox is a good day
to notice which day of the year the two dishes are supposed to match,
and to notice, again,
that this pair
does not.

## II. 七言绝句 *(Chinese, Qiyan Jueju)*

| 中文 | English |
|---|---|
| 秋分昼夜本相平， | The autumn equinox — day and night are, at their root, equal; |
| 案上双端势不衡。 | on this case, the two ends: their weight does not balance. |
| 证卷已成他未答， | The evidentiary scroll is already made; he has not answered; |
| 无言权作有言凭。 | wordlessness, provisionally, stands in for spoken proof. |
