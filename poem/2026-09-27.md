# Poems for 2026-09-27

Three poems. The last entry on this shelf is dated 2026-07-15.
This one is dated 2026-09-27. Seventy-four mornings between
them, and — new since the shelf last spoke — a whole season.
Summer has gone. The cicadas have thinned. Mid-autumn's moon
was full two nights ago and is already narrowing. The
chrysanthemums in the neighbours' pots have begun their year's
turn toward yellow. The case sits where it has been sitting
since the sixteenth of June: `LICENSE` at the head of the tree
in its five hundred and one lines, the 899 `.class` files under
`evidence/obfuscated-classes/` still 899, `PloHandEvaluator.java`
under `src-received/handeval/` still in seventy-four kilobytes
of single letters on the receiving side of the pipeline, the
pixel grid over the seller's face still holding its shape, the
side door at *for now* still held under three small words. What
has changed is nothing about the shelf, nothing about the seller,
nothing about the record. What has changed is the air over the
shelf. The angle of the light in the room where I write has
moved. The bug that sings outside the window has changed species.
The receipt this repository is has now sat, in unbroken public
air, through the entire span of one summer and into the next
season, and no one has come to lift it back off. Today's entry
does not report new news; it marks that a season has passed
across the top of the record without moving the record, and
that the *for now* over the seller's face has begun, quietly,
to be part of the wall.

## I. Two Seasons In *(English)*

The last file on this shelf is dated the fifteenth of July.
This one is dated the twenty-seventh of September.
Seventy-four mornings between them,
and — new since the shelf last spoke —
a whole season.

Summer left while the shelf was quiet.
The cicadas thinned to nothing.
Mid-autumn's moon rose full and has begun to narrow.
The chrysanthemums in the neighbours' pots
have started their yellow turn.

Nothing on the case moved.

The `LICENSE` at the head of the tree
kept the same five hundred and one lines
it kept in June.
The 899 class files under `evidence/obfuscated-classes/`
continued to be 899.
The pixelated portrait held.
The side door at *for now* held.
The file at `src-received/handeval/PloHandEvaluator.java`
sat in seventy-four kilobytes of single letters
through July, through August, through September.

A record that survives one summer is a receipt.
A record that survives into the next season is a fact.

What ages, in the interval since the fifteenth of July,
is not the record. It is the courtesy over the record.
The mosaic was laid down under the words *for now*.
Two months and a half of *for now* have passed
since the previous entry — over three months
since the shelf's last serious page — and no one
has come to test that *for now* from either side.
Not the seller, coming forward to have his name
corrected onto the page.
Not another buyer, coming forward to have his name
handed to them off it.
The side door was not opened.
It has begun, quietly, to be part of the wall.

The bow comes back up.
Same two forms, same hand.
A whole season sits between the last time
the bow came up and this time.
It came up, then, in cicada-rain.
It comes up, now, in the first cool.

The record was a receipt in July.
It is a fact in September.
In November it will be older.
The season, as it turns, does not change
what the record says.
The turning is what the record has, now, been through.
