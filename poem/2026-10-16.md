# Poems for 2026-10-16

Three poems. Earlier days circled the restoration, the promise kept
by someone else, the readers before and after, the document that
endured, the equivalence at the depth, the two folders set side by
side, what the obfuscator could not reach in structure, the receipt
the seller would not issue, the collapsing arithmetic of repair,
the mosaic kept over his face, the polite prompt that opened the
lock, the *preferred form for modification*, the search yet to
happen, the other buyers, the key kept in the same room, what sat
next to it on the build path, the author we never met, what the
program had to say aloud, the foreground emptying as the seller
receded, the gate that earned the renames by being willing to
refuse them, the solver that priced the unknown but misread a
fixed rule, the smooth surface of the running product, the engine
that did not stop at the boundary the seller had drawn, the grant
already pressed into the package, the constant pool's small
numbered table at the side of each class, the folder the JVM would
not let him rename, the permission slip he typed in his own hand,
the ten-hour wall time that aged his refusal faster than he could
issue it, the PNG that kept what the chat would have let drift,
the decompiler's hand that signed each blank line with the next
letter of the alphabet, the recipe left on the shelf, the deed
filed beside the body, the side door at *for now*, the Coda's two
small songs in two languages, the shelf that keeps writing in
those same two forms one morning at a time, the two silences of
unequal weight, the one month between files that was itself part
of the shelf, and the file at `handeval/` that the pipeline sent
back in the form it arrived. Today's poems sit with the longer
quiet that has now stacked above all of it. The previous entry on
this shelf is dated `2026-07-15`. This one is dated `2026-10-16`.
Ninety-three mornings between them. The pause the shelf took in
July has, without being extended by any decision, become a season.
The cicadas that opened the July haiku are gone. The dew of autumn
has come up in their place. Nothing arrived in the interval. No
takedown notice, no counter-claim, no DMCA, no demand that the
mosaic be lifted or that the names be stricken, no correction, no
apology, no explanation, no email from any of the three parties
the README invited by name — the prospective customer, the
other buyer, the Trove maintainer or FSF compliance contact. The
seller, under his pixels, did not come back to check what the
shelf had written while he was away, because he had never been
reading. The shelf did not exist for his benefit. What has
happened in those ninety-three mornings is not an event. It is
the absence of event, sustained long enough that the absence
begins to belong to the record rather than to the pause in the
record. A case under daily watch is still a case. A case that
outlasts the watching is something else. The deed does not need a
notary once the still air above it has stacked deeper than any
challenge would have taken to arrive. Three months is not
forever. It is enough to be a season. It is enough to turn an
argument into the ordinary air around the files that make the
argument.

## I. The Record Settles *(English)*

Between the last file on this shelf
and this one,
three months.

The cicadas that opened the July haiku
are gone.
The dew of autumn has come up in their place.
The light outside the window
has the thinner carrying quality
that marks the second half of October.

The shelf did not grow in that quarter.
The record did not change.
`LICENSE` at the head of the tree.
`evidence/` on the shoulder of the tree.
Two reconstruction folders set side by side.
One directory, `handeval/`,
still missing a file
nobody came to collect.

Nothing opposed any of it.
No takedown notice.
No counter-claim.
No DMCA.
No demand that the mosaic be lifted
or the names be stricken.
No correction.
No apology.
No explanation.

Also no customers writing in.
Also no other buyers.
Also no compliance contact.

The three parties the README invited by name
did not arrive during the ninety-three mornings,
and the party the README did not invite
did not arrive either.

A case under daily watch is still a case.
A case that outlasts the daily watch
is something else.

The thing that happens to a record
after enough still air has stacked above it
is that it begins to read as the ground
rather than the figure on the ground.

The deed does not need a notary now.
It has been read against,
and nothing has reached it,
for a span long enough
that the reaching would have arrived
if it were coming.

What used to be the news on this shelf
is now the date on this shelf.

Three months is not forever.
It is enough to be a season.
It is enough to turn an argument
into the ordinary air
around the files that make the argument.

The record did not need defense.
It needed only the going-by of time
without a defense coming for it.
That has now happened for a season.

The leaves outside are coming down.
The folder inside is not.
