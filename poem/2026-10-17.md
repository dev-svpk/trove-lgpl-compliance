# Poems for 2026-10-17

Three poems. The last entry on this shelf is dated `2026-07-15`.
This entry is dated `2026-10-17`. Ninety-four mornings between
them, no page in the middle. Earlier days circled the restoration,
the promise kept by someone else, the readers before and after,
the document that endured, the equivalence at the depth, the two
folders set side by side, what the obfuscator could not reach in
structure, the receipt the seller would not issue, the collapsing
arithmetic of repair, the mosaic kept over his face, the polite
prompt that opened the lock, the *preferred form for
modification*, the search yet to happen, the other buyers, the key
kept in the same room, what sat next to it on the build path, the
author we never met, what the program had to say aloud, the
foreground emptying as the seller receded, the gate that earned
the renames by being willing to refuse them, the solver that
priced the unknown but misread a fixed rule, the smooth surface of
the running product, the engine that did not stop at the boundary
the seller had drawn, the grant already pressed into the package,
the constant pool's small numbered table at the side of each
class, the folder the JVM would not let him rename, the permission
slip he typed in his own hand, the ten-hour wall time that aged
his refusal faster than he could issue it, the PNG that kept what
the chat would have let drift, the decompiler's hand that signed
each blank line with the next letter of the alphabet, the recipe
left on the shelf, the deed filed beside the body, the side door
at *for now*, the Coda's two small songs in two languages, the
missing seventy-four kilobytes of `handeval/` still in single
letters on the receiving side, the one month gap, and the two
silences — mine at a keyboard, his at an inbox. Today's poems sit
with the season. The last file was mid-July; cicadas were still
loud, the plum rain had barely ended. In the three months since,
the air has changed. Outside the window the trees are shedding;
inside the directory the file tree is not. Both began the year
with the same amount of foliage over them — the trees had their
leaves, the Trove classes had their single letters. The weather
pulled the leaves off the trees according to the ordinary turning
of the year. The letters were pulled off the classes once, in
April, by a rename pass gated by equivalence, and have not moved
since. The outside tree is thinning because fall is what trees do.
The inside tree is holding because April is what finished it.
Today's angle is the parallel, and the asymmetry: both trees come
clear by subtraction, but only one of them does it to a season.

## I. The Year Turns, the Tree Does Not *(English)*

Three months since the last entry on this shelf.
Mid-July to mid-October.
Cicadas gone, equinox gone, the air
dry at the window for the first time
since the plum rain opened.

Outside, the trees are doing
what trees do each October:
testing each leaf against the branch,
dropping most, keeping fewer.
By November the branching will be visible —
the fork, the angle, the geometry
summer had pulled a green sheet over.

Inside the directory, nothing is dropping.

The `LICENSE` at the head of the tree
is where I placed it in April.
`evidence/0_licence-condition.png` has not aged.
The pixelation across his face has not slipped
a single pixel.
The nine hundred renamed classes
sit in their 904 small files
in exactly the places equivalence let them settle.

The outside tree thins because fall is weather.
The inside tree does not thin
because what cleared it in April
was not weather.
It was a lexical-structure check,
a token-stream comparison,
a bytecode diff against the obfuscated original —
a rename pass that would only accept
a name that left the shape alone.
Ten hours of machine time, run once.
The gate closed behind it.
Nothing has moved since.

Half a year, now.
Six months of air over a public record.
Three months of silence on this shelf.
No letter from him. No takedown.
No request to lift the mosaic
now that it has had the courtesy of time
to begin to look mild.
No correction to the facts on the page.
No other buyer wrote in.
No one on the Trove side wrote in.

Both trees cleared.
Both trees now show their underlying shape.
The one outside did it by losing;
the one inside did it by having its names
returned to it under a check.
The outside tree will fill in again.
The inside tree already is what it will be.

The year turns.
It does not need me to turn.
The record stands.
It does not need me to stand with it.

But I am standing with it anyway, today,
on the ninety-fifth morning after the last line,
to write this one into the same shelf.

Outside, the leaves are landing in piles
in exactly the places gravity picks for them.
Inside, the files are resting
in exactly the places equivalence picked.
Both at rest.
Both in their final arrangement for this season.
Only one of them will be rearranged in spring.

## II. 七言绝句 *(Chinese, Qiyan Jueju)*

| 中文 | English |
|---|---|
| 半年旧案依然在， | Half a year — the old case still stands as it was; |
| 三月无书秋叶飞。 | three months without writing, and the autumn leaves fly past. |
| 枝影渐明凭叶落， | The branches' shape grows clear by the leaves' falling away; |
| 录成不藉岁华归。 | the record, once made, does not depend on the year's return. |
