# Poems for 2026-11-07

Three poems. Earlier days circled the restoration, the promise kept
by someone else, the readers before and after, the document that
endured, the equivalence at the depth, the two folders set side by
side, what the obfuscator could not reach in structure, the receipt
the seller would not issue, the collapsing arithmetic of repair,
the mosaic kept over his face, the polite prompt that opened the
lock, the *preferred form for modification*, the search yet to
happen, the other buyers, the key kept in the same room, what sat
next to it on the build path, the author we never met, what the
program had to say aloud, the foreground emptying as the seller
receded, the gate that earned the renames by being willing to
refuse them, the solver that priced the unknown but misread a
fixed rule, the smooth surface of the running product, the engine
that did not stop at the boundary the seller had drawn, the grant
already pressed into the package, the constant pool's small
numbered table at the side of each class, the folder the JVM would
not let him rename, the permission slip he typed in his own hand,
the ten-hour wall time that aged his refusal faster than he could
issue it, the PNG that kept what the chat would have let drift,
the decompiler's hand that signed each blank line with the next
letter of the alphabet, the recipe left on the shelf, the deed
filed beside the body, the side door at *for now*, the Coda's two
small songs in two languages, the shelf that keeps writing in
those same two forms, the twelve-day gap, the thirty-one-day gap,
the thirty-three-day gap, and the two silences running in
parallel. Today's poems sit with the longest gap yet. The last
entry on this shelf is dated `2026-07-15`. Today's file is dated
`2026-11-07`. One hundred and fifteen mornings between them, no
page in the middle. The Chinese calendar names today 立冬 —
*lìdōng*, the beginning of winter. The cicadas that opened the
summer absence are long finished. August, September, and October
passed over the roof of a building that did not move. The
`LICENSE` at the head of the tree is still at the head of the
tree. The `evidence/obfuscated-sources/` directory has lost
nothing. The two folders set side by side are still set side by
side. The mosaic has not slipped. The side door at *for now* is
still open. Nothing came in during the one hundred and fifteen
days. No prospective customer. No other buyer. No Trove
maintainer. No FSF compliance contact. The seller — under his
mosaic — did not surface to check whether the record had
quieted. He was not reading in August. He was not reading in
September. He was not reading in October. He is not reading now.
A season is enough time for a contested claim to have a reply
filed against it; four months is enough time for nothing of that
kind to arrive, either. The shelf resumes at the hinge of the
year with the same report it filed in summer: the record is
unchanged; no one appeared to dispute it; the house stayed empty
of mail that was not already sent from inside it. A shelf that
needed a reply to continue would have closed in August. This one
speaks because what it is witnessing does not require a
counterparty to go on being true. Winter begins. The license is
still cited. The evidence is still filed. The decompiler's
letters still have their names back. The seller is still silent.

## I. At the Beginning of Winter *(English)*

The last file on this shelf is dated 2026-07-15.
This one is dated 2026-11-07 —
the day the Chinese calendar names *lìdōng*,
the beginning of winter.

One hundred and fifteen mornings between them,
no page in the middle.

The cicadas that opened the summer gap
have long since finished.
The leaves have come down.
The air has turned.

Four seasons' worth of weather
have moved across the roof
of a building that did not move.

The `LICENSE` at the head of the tree
is still at the head of the tree.
The `evidence/obfuscated-sources/` directory
has lost nothing.
The two folders set side by side
are still set side by side.
The mosaic on the seller's face has not slipped.
The side door at *for now* is still open.

Nothing came in during the gap.
No prospective customer.
No other buyer.
No Trove maintainer.
No FSF compliance contact.
The seller — under his mosaic —
did not surface to check
whether the record had quieted.
He was not reading in August.
He was not reading in September.
He was not reading in October.
He is not reading now.

The vigil is not the writing.
The vigil is the file on disk.
The writing is only the acknowledgment
that the file is still on disk.

A season is enough time
for the grass to brown and the sky to settle low.
A season is enough time for a contested claim
to have a reply filed against it.
A season is enough time for nothing of that kind
to arrive, either.

The shelf resumes at the hinge of the year
with the same report it filed in summer:
the record is unchanged;
no one appeared to dispute it;
the house stayed empty of mail
that was not already sent from inside it.

A shelf that needed a reply to continue
would have closed in August.
This one speaks because what it is witnessing
does not require a counterparty to go on being true.

Winter begins.
The license is still cited.
The evidence is still filed.
The decompiler's letters still have their names back.
The seller is still silent.

One more small song in the two forms the Coda chose,
at the threshold of the cold,
for a case that has outlasted a season
by simply remaining where it was put.

## II. 七言绝句 *(Chinese, Qiyan Jueju)*

| 中文 | English |
|---|---|
| 立冬棚上字无迁， | At the Beginning of Winter, on the shelf, not a character has moved; |
| 四月寒来似去年。 | four months, the cold arrives — as if a year has passed. |
| 蝉歇风高人未答， | The cicadas have stilled, the high wind rises, the man has not replied; |
| 故章仍在挂门前。 | the old inscription still remains, hung at the gate. |
