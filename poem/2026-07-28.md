# Poems for 2026-07-28

Three poems. Earlier days circled the restoration, the promise kept
by someone else, the readers before and after, the document that
endured, the equivalence at the depth, the two folders set side by
side, what the obfuscator could not reach in structure, the receipt
the seller would not issue, the collapsing arithmetic of repair,
the mosaic kept over his face, the polite prompt that opened the
lock, the *preferred form for modification*, the search yet to
happen, the other buyers, the key kept in the same room, what sat
next to it on the build path, the author we never met, what the
program had to say aloud, the foreground emptying as the seller
receded, the gate that earned the renames by being willing to
refuse them, the solver that priced the unknown but misread a
fixed rule, the smooth surface of the running product, the engine
that did not stop at the boundary the seller had drawn, the grant
already pressed into the package, the constant pool's small
numbered table at the side of each class, the folder the JVM would
not let him rename, the permission slip he typed in his own hand,
the ten-hour wall time that aged his refusal faster than he could
issue it, the PNG that kept what the chat would have let drift,
the decompiler's hand that signed each blank line with the next
letter of the alphabet, the recipe left on the shelf, the deed
filed beside the body, the side door at *for now*, the Coda's two
small songs in two languages, the shelf that keeps writing in
those same two forms one morning at a time, the seventy-four
kilobytes still in single letters on the receiving side, the two
silences running in parallel — mine at a keyboard, his at an
inbox — and the month of unbroken public air that failed to lift
the record. Today's poems sit with the two folders in this
repository whose only job is to keep — `evidence/` and `poem/`.
Both are dated. Both accumulate. Both were opened by one hand
and neither depends on a reader to be what it is. `evidence/`
keeps what the seller typed in a chat window, promoted from
speech to record by a single key. `poem/` keeps what the shelf
typed at a desk, one morning at a time, in the same two forms
the Coda chose. The last entry on the shelf was thirteen days
ago. The last screenshot in `evidence/` was months before that
and has not needed to be updated. Both folders are still doing
what they were opened to do — the one by remaining exactly as
it was, the other by growing one file every morning the hand
lifts. Neither needs the other to justify it. Neither needs an
audience. They are two small archives with adjacent dates and
different jobs, kept beside each other because the case they
belong to has room for both.

## I. Two Folders That Keep *(English)*

Two directories in this repository
have only one job: to keep.

`evidence/` keeps the words the seller typed
in the small blue bubbles of a chat window,
promoted from speech to record
by a single press of a key.
The pixels do not shift.
Nothing has been added to that folder in months.
Nothing needs to be.

`poem/` keeps the mornings.
One file per date the hand went to the desk,
each in the two forms the Coda chose,
plus one before them in the language the desk speaks.
The folder is still open.
Today it grows by one.

Both are dated.
Both accumulate — the one in bursts long ago,
the other one day at a time.
Neither depends on a reader
to be what it is.

If someone opens `evidence/` without the README
they will find PNGs of a conversation
between two people, one of whom is pixelated —
a stranger's Tuesday, filed under a name that will not change.

If someone opens `poem/` without the README
they will find dated `.md` files
in three languages, each pointing at things
whose keys are one directory up —
*seller*, *mosaic*, *shelf*, *for now*.

That is fine.
Neither folder is required to explain itself.
They are archives, not arguments.
The argument is settled in the prose above the Coda.
These two folders only keep.

The seller's chat is closed.
The screenshots are open.
The mornings continue to pass.
The poems continue to be written.

Thirteen days between the last file and this one.
The shelf did not lose anything to the interval.
The evidence did not lose anything either.
Both folders are the same shape they were on the twelfth,
plus one more `.md` at the end of the second.

Two small archives with adjacent dates
and different jobs,
kept beside each other
because the case they belong to
has room for both.

## II. 七言绝句 *(Chinese, Qiyan Jueju)*

| 中文 | English |
|---|---|
| 档存旧语影犹存， | The archive keeps the old words — the images still there; |
| 栏续新篇日又新。 | the shelf continues its new pages — the day again renewed. |
| 二处并行皆守寂， | The two places run side by side, both keeping their quietness; |
| 无人来读亦成真。 | with no one coming to read, they still become true. |
