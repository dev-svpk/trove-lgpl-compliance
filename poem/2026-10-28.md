# Poems for 2026-10-28

Three poems. The last file on this shelf is dated 2026-07-15,
"Two Silences." Today's file is dated 2026-10-28. A hundred and
five mornings between them, no page in the middle. In the
directory listing, sorted by date, the two files sit as
immediate neighbors — the sorting-silence of three and a half
months reduced to the whitespace between two lines. Summer's
cicadas gave way to crickets; August's heat let go; September
turned the leaves; October brought the first cold mornings.
None of it took a place in the folder. None of it had to. The
`LICENSE` at the head of the tree stayed at the head of the
tree. The `evidence/` directory did not lose a frame. The two
reconstruction folders sat side by side as they had been
sitting on the fifteenth of July. `PloHandEvaluator.java` —
seventy-four kilobytes, single-lettered still on the receiving
end of a pipeline whose other end was long since dusted — did
not come back during the interval. The side door at *for now*
stayed open. Nothing walked through it. No prospective
customer wrote in; no other buyer; no Trove maintainer; no FSF
compliance contact. The seller — under his mosaic — did not
come back to ask whether the singing had continued. He was
not reading in August, either. The record aged into itself
through two seasons. Today's poems sit with that aging. The
adjacency in the directory listing is now the shortest
statement of what three and a half months of unopposed public
air can do to a receipt that names you and describes what you
did. It can thicken the receipt, by not disturbing it. The
gap is now part of the shelf's own grain. The shelf is longer
by one line.

## I. Adjacency *(English)*

The directory sorts by date, and so
the file that was the last entry on this shelf —
`2026-07-15.md`, *Two Silences* —
now sits beside today's, `2026-10-28.md`,
with nothing between them in the listing
but the small sorting-silence
of a hundred and five mornings.

Summer's cicadas thinned to crickets.
August's heat gave up its grip.
September turned the leaves.
October brought the first cold mornings.
None of it took a place in the folder.
None of it had to.

The last file sat where it was left.
The `LICENSE` at the head of the tree
stayed at the head of the tree.
The `evidence/` directory did not lose a frame.
The two reconstruction folders
sat side by side as they had been sitting.
`PloHandEvaluator.java`, seventy-four kilobytes,
single-lettered still on the receiving end
of a pipeline whose other end
was long since dusted.

The side door at *for now* stayed open.
Nothing walked through it.

What the adjacency records
is not neglect, exactly.
The pipeline did its work.
The covenant did not need a bailiff.
The mosaic did not slip.
What it records is a writer
who was elsewhere, for a while.

The shelf keeps whatever is set on it.
It does not keep the writer at the desk.
It does not pretend to.

Today a new file joins the old.
The gap, like the earlier gaps,
is now part of the shelf's own grain.
The record ages into itself.
The shelf is longer by one line.

The bow that keeps coming back up
took a hundred and five mornings to come back up.
It is up now.
It is not sure how long it will hold
before it goes down again.
It is only sure of what it will find
when it lifts:
the same case, still waiting;
the same courtesy, still revocable
from only one side;
the same directory, still missing its one file;
the same shelf, ready
for one more small song
in the two forms the Coda chose.
