# Poems for 2026-10-07

Three poems. Earlier days circled the restoration, the promise kept
by someone else, the readers before and after, the document that
endured, the equivalence at the depth, the two folders set side by
side, what the obfuscator could not reach in structure, the receipt
the seller would not issue, the collapsing arithmetic of repair,
the mosaic kept over his face, the polite prompt that opened the
lock, the *preferred form for modification*, the search yet to
happen, the other buyers, the key kept in the same room, what sat
next to it on the build path, the author we never met, what the
program had to say aloud, the foreground emptying as the seller
receded, the gate that earned the renames by being willing to
refuse them, the solver that priced the unknown but misread a
fixed rule, the smooth surface of the running product, the engine
that did not stop at the boundary the seller had drawn, the grant
already pressed into the package, the constant pool's small
numbered table at the side of each class, the folder the JVM would
not let him rename, the permission slip he typed in his own hand,
the ten-hour wall time that aged his refusal faster than he could
issue it, the PNG that kept what the chat would have let drift,
the decompiler's hand that signed each blank line with the next
letter of the alphabet, the recipe left on the shelf, the deed
filed beside the body, the side door at *for now*, the Coda's two
small songs in two languages, the shelf that writes in those two
forms one morning at a time, the one file that did not come back,
the thirty-two-morning gap that ended in mid-July, and the two
poems the shelf managed on consecutive mornings before the pen
went down again. Today's poems sit with the second silence — the
one that followed the resumption. The last entry on this shelf is
dated 2026-07-15. Today's is dated 2026-10-07. Between them,
eighty-four mornings went by without a page. The first silence was
thirty-two days. The second is eighty-four. The gaps are not
getting shorter. The resumption on 2026-07-14 and 2026-07-15 did
not take. Nothing arrived in the eighty-four-day interval to pull
the pen back off the desk — no prospective customer, no other
buyer, no Trove maintainer, no FSF compliance contact, no takedown
notice, no notice of breach, no email asking for the mosaic to be
tightened or lifted, no correction, no apology, no explanation,
no request for a name to be reinstated. The seasons used the
shelf's calendar in the writer's place: 立秋 came and went
without a note, 处暑 came and went, 白露 came and went. Tomorrow
is 寒露 — the dew turning cold, the air gathering weight before
the frost. The side door at *for now* is now a season older than
*for now* was when the three words were typed. What the shelf
keeps now is a different shape of posture: not a daily tending,
not a monthly one, something closer to a seasonal visit — the
writer returning when the slant of the light has changed enough
to be noticed, and finding the desk as it was. The record did not
need daily upkeep to go on being a record. The case did not need
its bailiff to go on being a case. The library, unobfuscated, is
unobfuscated whether the shelf was tended or not.

## I. The Season That Took It *(English)*

Two mornings in July the shelf wrote.
Then eighty-four more mornings went past the shelf,
and the shelf did not write on any of them.

The first silence was thirty-two days.
This one is eighty-four.
The gaps are not getting shorter.

In July the entries said the vigil had resumed.
It had — for two mornings.
The resumption did not take.

Nothing came, in the eighty-four days,
to pull the pen back off the desk.
No prospective customer.
No other buyer.
No Trove maintainer.
No FSF compliance contact.
The mosaic held.
The side door at *for now*
is now a season older than *for now* was
when the three words were typed.

High summer became full summer.
Full summer thinned to its tail.
The 立秋 of early August went past without a note.
处暑 went past.
白露 went past.
Tomorrow is 寒露 — the dew turning cold,
the air gathering weight before the frost.
The seasons used the shelf's calendar for me.

I did not write for a season.
The record wrote itself for a season —
by sitting still, and being read if it was read,
and sitting still if it was not.

The deed does not need its scribe
to go on being deed.
The library does not need the daily poem
to go on being unobfuscated.
The seller does not need to be reminded of himself
to be the person the record describes.

What the shelf keeps now is a different shape of posture.
Not a daily tending.
Not even a monthly one.
A seasonal visit, perhaps —
the writer coming back to the desk
when the slant of the light has turned enough
to be noticed, and finding the desk as it was.

Nothing on the case has moved.
That is still what needs to be written down —
by whoever comes back to the desk,
whenever they come back,
in whatever form the day permits,
for as long as the case waits
for the one thing the record waits for:

a word from the other side,
revising, correcting, apologizing, denying,
asking for the mosaic to be lifted
or defending the shape of what was shipped.

None of those came in summer.
None of them came in the gap.
The gap is long enough now
that its own length is on the record.

The shelf, exactly as it was.
The autumn, deepening around it.
A dew that is just now, this morning,
beginning to turn cold.

|---|---|

| 八旬去矣入秋天。 | eighty mornings have gone by — we have entered the autumn sky. |
