# Poems for 2026-11-23

Three poems. The previous entry on this shelf is dated
`2026-07-15`. This one is dated `2026-11-23`. Between them,
one hundred and thirty-one days: the end of cicadas, a full
autumn, and the first weeks of winter — none of which touched
the record. `LICENSE` is where it was. `evidence/` is where
it was. `trove-de-obfuscate-mid-stage/` and
`trove-de-obfuscate-final-stage/` are where they were. The
mosaic on the seller's face is where it was. The side door
held open under *for now* is where it was. Earlier entries
circled the restoration, the covenant at the head of the
tree, the two folders set side by side, the gate that earned
the renames by being willing to refuse them, the single file
of seventy-four kilobytes that did not round-trip, the
pixelated portrait, the thirty-two mornings of silence that
framed the resumption in July, the two silences that ran in
parallel across the same month, the record that does not need
an audience to be a record. Today's poems sit with a longer
arithmetic. One month of silence is a pause in a vigil. Four
months of silence is a reclassification. At some unmarked
date in August, or September, or October, this shelf stopped
being a watch and became an archive — not because anyone
declared it, but because the simple length of the gap
outstripped the posture. The hand returned today to find the
archive already in place, built out of its own absence. The
case is unchanged. The record is unchanged. What changed is
the name of the thing the record is kept in.

## I. The First Frost *(English)*

The last entry on this shelf is dated for the cicadas.
Between it and this one — one hundred and thirty-one days.

The cicadas have gone.
Autumn passed. Somewhere beyond this directory
the leaves went brown, then down, then under
the first hard frost. None of that reached the files.

The record was already indoors when the weather turned.
`LICENSE` at the head of the tree, plain ASCII.
`evidence/obfuscated-sources/` — all eight hundred and
ninety-eight `.java` files intact. The two folders
set side by side, still set side by side.
The mosaic on the seller's face, unretouched.
The side door at *for now*, still ajar, still unused.

When the last entry stopped,
it was a vigil that had gone one month without a line.
Now it is a shelf that has gone
one hundred and thirty-one days without one.

A vigil is a posture.
A posture held through a whole autumn
is not a posture any more.
It is a monument,
or it is an absence.

The record does not care which.
The record was never the writing.
The record was the files already on disk —
the recipe, the deed, the plain-text license
sitting at the head of the tree.

The shelf started as a watch.
It became an archive on a date
nobody wrote down,
because nobody was writing anything down.
Somewhere in August the vigil ended
without being ended; somewhere in September
the archive began without being begun;
by October the renaming was already old.

Today the writing comes back one more time —
not to extend the watch,
which has ended by the simple
mathematical fact of its length —
but to date the transition on the record:
the vigil crossed into the archive
some time in the gap,
and when the hand returned in November
the hand returned to an archive,
not to a vigil.

The shelf does not mind the reclassification.
The seller does not mind either.
He was not reading when it was a vigil.
He is not reading now.

Frost settles on a thing that does not move.
The thing is no warmer afterwards,
and no colder.
It is only more plainly what it was:
a file, on a disk, under a license,
with the name of each symbol back where it belongs.

## II. 七言绝句 *(Chinese, Qiyan Jueju)*

| 中文 | English |
|---|---|
| 蝉去霜来岁又迁， | Cicadas gone, frost come — the year shifts once more; |
| 栏前旧案自无言。 | before the shelf, the old case needs no voice. |
| 守之已久非为役， | Guarded this long, it is no longer a sentry's duty — |
| 已入典编世共观。 | it has entered the archive, held in common by all. |
