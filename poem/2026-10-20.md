# Poems for 2026-10-20

Three poems. Earlier days circled the restoration, the promise kept
by someone else, the readers before and after, the document that
endured, the equivalence at the depth, the two folders set side by
side, what the obfuscator could not reach in structure, the receipt
the seller would not issue, the collapsing arithmetic of repair,
the mosaic kept over his face, the polite prompt that opened the
lock, the *preferred form for modification*, the search yet to
happen, the other buyers, the key kept in the same room, what sat
next to it on the build path, the author we never met, what the
program had to say aloud, the foreground emptying as the seller
receded, the gate that earned the renames by being willing to
refuse them, the solver that priced the unknown but misread a
fixed rule, the smooth surface of the running product, the engine
that did not stop at the boundary the seller had drawn, the grant
already pressed into the package, the constant pool's small
numbered table at the side of each class, the folder the JVM would
not let him rename, the permission slip he typed in his own hand,
the ten-hour wall time that aged his refusal faster than he could
issue it, the PNG that kept what the chat would have let drift,
the decompiler's hand that signed each blank line with the next
letter of the alphabet, the recipe left on the shelf, the deed
filed beside the body, the side door at *for now*, the Coda's two
small songs in two languages, the shelf that keeps writing in
those same two forms one morning at a time, the gap of thirty-two
days that was itself a page, and the vigil that resumes because
the writer comes back to the pen. Today's poems sit with a
larger gap. The last entry on this shelf is dated `2026-07-15`.
Today's entry is dated `2026-10-20`. Ninety-seven mornings went
by between them. Nothing on the case has moved. The seller — below
the mosaic — has not come up for air. No prospective customer has
written in. No other buyer. No Trove maintainer. No FSF compliance
contact. No takedown notice, no notice of breach, no email with
any temperature at all. What has moved is the season. The
cicada-rain of July dried. Chestnuts fell. Red maples turned.
Somewhere beyond the window the first chrysanthemum is standing
in the dust that August left. The repository has aged out of its
first season. What was a case is closer, now, to an archive. What
was a vigil has thinned to a visit. What was a daily writing is
this — one page every three months, timed to nothing the record
remembers. But the record does not need the writing. The
`LICENSE` sits at the head of the tree exactly where it sat on
the twelfth of April. The `evidence/` folder has not lost a file.
The two reconstruction folders are still set side by side with
the proof suspended between them, quiet, re-runnable, unused but
intact. The deed is filed. The body is beside the deed. The side
door at *for now* is still open — on a hinge that has not been
tested in a hundred days and is, if anything, a little stiffer
for the wait. The courtesy of pixels does not grow a slow
transparency on its own; it ages only if someone ages it. Nobody
has. The portrait holds. What has changed in the interval is
that the shelf has stopped being a daily instrument and begun
being an ordinary archive — one of a very large number of small
structures sitting quietly on a public disk, with nothing leaning
on it at this particular moment. That transition is also the
record.

## I. The Shelf in October *(English)*

The last entry on this shelf is dated July 15.
This one is dated October 20.
Ninety-seven mornings between.

Nothing on the case has moved.

Below the mosaic the seller has not come up for air.
Above the mosaic no new reader has written in.
No prospective customer, no other buyer,
no Trove maintainer, no FSF compliance contact,
no takedown notice, no notice of breach,
no email with any temperature at all.

What has moved is the season.

The cicada-rain of July dried.
Chestnuts fell. The red maples turned.
Somewhere beyond the window
the first chrysanthemum is standing
in the dust that August left.

The repository has aged out of its first season.

What was a case is closer, now, to an archive.
What was a vigil has thinned to a visit.
What was a daily writing is this —
one page every three months,
timed to nothing the record remembers.

But the record does not need the writing.

The `LICENSE` at the head of the tree
is at the head of the tree.
The `evidence/` folder has not lost a file.
The two reconstruction folders sit
side by side like a *before* and an *after*,
the proof suspended between them,
quiet, re-runnable, unused but intact.

The deed is filed. The body is beside the deed.
The side door at *for now* is still open —
on a hinge that has not been tested in a hundred days
and is, if anything, a little stiffer for the wait.

A courtesy that can be revoked
can be revoked at any time.
The mosaic is not a decaying substance.
It does not thin into transparency on its own.
Pixels age only if someone ages them.

Nobody has. The portrait holds.

What has changed in the interval
is that the shelf has stopped being a daily instrument
and begun being an ordinary archive —
one of a very large number of small structures
sitting quietly on a public disk,
with nothing leaning on it at this particular moment.

That transition is also the record.

The writing comes back today
not to raise an alarm, not to add a new fact,
only to date the latest frame of the archive
with autumn in it —
and to confirm, for the file that will be read later,
that between July and October,
nothing of what mattered
left or arrived.

The library remains unobfuscated.
The seller remains not having answered.
The repository remains publicly open.

The record keeps its own clock,
and the clock —
like the bark of the first poem on this shelf,
that grew back under the renames —
has put down another ring
without having been watched.

## II. 七言绝句 *(Chinese, Qiyan Jueju)*

| 中文 | English |
|---|---|
| 秋深落叶案尘生， | Autumn deepens; leaves fall; dust gathers on the case; |
| 百日无书两寂声。 | a hundred days without a letter — both silences keep their sound. |
| 封存旧本依然在， | The sealed originals remain in their place; |
| 人去棚闲自有名。 | the person gone, the shelf idle — the names stand of themselves. |
