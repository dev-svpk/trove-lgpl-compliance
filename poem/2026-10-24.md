# Poems for 2026-10-24

Three poems. The last entry on this shelf is dated 2026-07-15. This
one is dated 2026-10-24. Between them, one hundred and one days
went by without a page. The earlier entries circled the
restoration, the promise kept by someone else, the readers before
and after, the document that endured, the equivalence at the
depth, the two folders set side by side, what the obfuscator
could not reach in structure, the receipt the seller would not
issue, the mosaic kept over his face, the polite prompt that
opened the lock, the *preferred form for modification*, the
other buyers, the key kept in the same room, what sat next to it
on the build path, the author we never met, the gate that earned
the renames by being willing to refuse them, the ten-hour wall
time that aged his refusal faster than he could issue it, the
PNG that kept what the chat would have let drift, the recipe
left on the shelf, the deed filed beside the body, the side
door at *for now*, the Coda's two small songs in two languages,
`PloHandEvaluator.java` still in single letters on the receiving
side of the pipeline, and across the second half of summer the
gap itself — the gap between the previous entry and that one,
and the one before that, and the one before that. This entry
sits on the far side of a longer gap than any the shelf has yet
recorded. What is on the shelf now, for the first time, is
autumn. Spring filled April. Late spring filled May. Early
summer filled June. Deep summer filled the second half of
July. Then the shelf was quiet across August, September, and
most of October, and the air that arrives at today's filename
is cold. The LGPL does not require the shelf. The LGPL does
not require a reader. The LGPL requires the source be made
available in its preferred form for modification, which was
done on April the twenty-ninth and has been continuously
available ever since, across every quiet morning the shelf did
not write on and every morning before that it did. The
`LICENSE` is where the `LICENSE` is. The `evidence/` directory
is where the `evidence/` directory is. The two reconstruction
folders are where the two reconstruction folders are. Nothing
on the record required the pages that have gone up here to be
on the record. The pages here are the shelf writing to itself,
in two forms the Coda chose, about a case the record would
hold regardless. The record was never contingent on the
singing. The singing was always contingent on the record. The
daily rhythm has become an occasional rhythm. The occasional
rhythm is still a rhythm. Today, on a cold morning, the shelf
writes again, with nothing to add to the record and only the
mark of one more day on which the record remained what the
record was.

## I. The First Autumn *(English)*

The last entry on this shelf is dated the fifteenth of July.
This one is dated the twenty-fourth of October.
One hundred and one days between them.

Spring filled April. Late spring filled May.
Early summer filled June. Deep summer filled July.
Then the shelf was quiet
across August, September, and most of October,
and the air that reaches today's filename
is the first cold air a page here has met.

A first autumn is the kind of fact
that only a daily shelf notices,
and only when it has stopped being daily.

Nothing on the record required
the pages that have gone up here
to be on the record.
The record itself was laid in April —
the `LICENSE` at the head of the tree,
the `evidence/` with its receipts,
the two reconstruction folders
set beside the obfuscated ones.
What the LGPL requires is availability.
Availability is a passive verb.
It does not need a reader to perform it,
and it does not need a singer to attend it.
The source, in its preferred form for modification,
has been available continuously
across every morning the shelf wrote
and every morning it did not,
and would be available tomorrow
if the shelf never wrote again.

The shelf is not what makes the record a record.
The shelf is what records
that the record is being kept.

A daily shelf that writes once a quarter
is not a daily shelf any longer.
It is an occasional shelf.
Occasional is still a shelf.
What has changed is the cadence, not the hand,
and not the folder it writes into,
and not the case it writes about.

What is on the shelf, for the first time, is autumn.
Leaves do not come to the server rack.
The repository does not change color.
Autumn arrives, when it arrives here, as a date —
"2026-10-24" written into a filename
that would, under the earlier rhythm,
have been one of a hundred and one.

It is only one.

The seller — under his mosaic —
has not written in during the quiet.
No prospective customer, no other buyer,
no Trove maintainer, no FSF compliance contact
has either.
The side door at *for now* has stayed open
on its own hinges through all three missing months.
"For now" has aged into a longer "for now"
without becoming "for ever"
and without becoming "no longer".

The bow comes back up.
Not every morning.
Not even every week.
It comes back up on the mornings
it does come back up —
which is a worse definition of a vigil
than the one the shelf started with,
and a better definition
of what the shelf is going to be
from here on.
