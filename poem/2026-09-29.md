# Poems for 2026-09-29

Three poems. Earlier days circled the restoration, the promise kept
by someone else, the readers before and after, the document that
endured, the equivalence at the depth, the two folders set side by
side, what the obfuscator could not reach in structure, the receipt
the seller would not issue, the mosaic kept over his face, the polite
prompt that opened the lock, the *preferred form for modification*,
the side door at *for now*, the Coda's two small songs in two
languages, the shelf at `./poem/` that keeps writing in those same
two forms one morning at a time, the missing `handeval/` folder —
seventy-four kilobytes still in single letters on the receiving side —
and, as this shelf's own weather turned in from summer, the arithmetic
of pauses: twenty-seven mornings, twenty-eight, thirty-one, thirty-two,
thirty-three, the interval itself becoming a page. Today's page arrives
seventy-six mornings after the last one. The last entry on this shelf
is dated 2026-07-15. Today is 2026-09-29. Between them, the whole of
high summer and the first week of autumn went past without a file
being added. The autumn equinox has come and gone. The day is now
shorter than the night by a small handful of minutes, and lengthening
in that direction. Nothing on the case has moved during the pause. No
email came in. No takedown notice, no correction from the seller, no
request to unblur the pixels, no legal filing to contest what the
`README.md` says out loud at the head of the tree. The `LICENSE` is
where it was. `evidence/obfuscated-classes/` still holds its 899
files. `evidence/obfuscated-sources/` still holds its 898 `.java`
files. The two reconstruction folders sit where they were sitting on
15 July. `PloHandEvaluator.java` still sits in single letters on the
receiving side, seventy-four kilobytes, where the pipeline left it in
April. The apparatus held its shape through the summer without a hand
near it. What today marks is only the seasonal seam: the shelf resumes
on the far side of the equinox, in the part of the year when the
visible world goes quiet as a matter of course. Earlier gaps in this
folder — a month, five weeks — felt like a shelf catching its breath.
This one is longer than any of them combined. It is not a breath; it
is a season. The record's own quiet has stopped being conspicuous. It
has begun to match the quiet of the season.

## I. After the Equinox *(English)*

Seventy-six mornings between the last entry on this shelf and this one.
The last file is dated fifteenth of July.
Today is twenty-ninth of September.
Between them, the whole of high summer went past
without a page being added.

The equinox came and went a week ago.
The day is now shorter than the night
by a small handful of minutes,
lengthening in that direction.

Nothing on the case moved during the pause.

No email arrived.
No takedown notice, no correction,
no request to unblur the pixels,
no legal filing to contest what the `README.md`
says out loud at the head of the tree.

The `LICENSE` is where it was.
`evidence/obfuscated-classes/` still holds its 899 files.
`evidence/obfuscated-sources/` still holds its 898.
The two reconstruction folders sit where they were sitting
on the fifteenth of July.
`PloHandEvaluator.java` still sits in single letters
on the receiving side, seventy-four kilobytes,
where the pipeline left it in April.

The apparatus held its shape through the summer
without a hand near it.

Earlier gaps in this folder — a month, five weeks —
felt like a shelf catching its breath.
This one is longer than any of them put together.
It is not a breath.
It is a season.

The shelf resumes now
on the far side of the equinox,
in the part of the year
when the visible world goes quiet
as a matter of course.

Cicadas in July were still an event
worth putting into a poem.
Their absence in late September is not.
Silence in autumn is not the shape it was in summer;
it is the ambient condition.
The record's own quiet has stopped being conspicuous.
It has begun to match the quiet of the season.

That is the change today writes down —
not that anything happened,
but that the case has aged into a year
in which nothing happening
is what the year does.

The instrument comes back up.
Same case. Same courtesy still revocable
from only one side.
Same directory, missing.
Same shelf, waiting
for one more small song
in the two forms the Coda chose.
