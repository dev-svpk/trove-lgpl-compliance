# Poems for 2026-09-25

Three poems. The last entry on this shelf is dated 2026-07-15. This
entry is dated 2026-09-25. Seventy-two mornings between them, no page
in the middle. Earlier days circled the restoration, the promise kept
by someone else, the receipt the seller would not issue, the mosaic
kept over his face, the *preferred form for modification*, the
polite prompt that opened the lock, the ten-hour wall time that aged
his refusal faster than he could issue it, the folder the JVM would
not let him rename, the side door at *for now*, the Coda's two small
songs in two languages, the shelf at `./poem/` that keeps writing in
those same two forms one morning at a time, and the one file that did
not come back — the seventy-four kilobytes of Pot Limit Omaha
arithmetic still in single letters on the receiving side. Between the
last entry and this one the autumn equinox came and went. Summer left
the record without being seen off; autumn arrived without being
received at the door. Nothing on the case has moved. `LICENSE` still
sits at the head of the tree. `evidence/` still holds its obfuscated
`.java` files verbatim. `handeval/PloHandEvaluator.java` still sits
in single letters, the one folder `src-reconstructed-by-claude/` does
not contain. The mosaic still holds his face. *For now* still holds
its side door. The seller has still not written — the silence has
now aged through a whole change of season. Equinox is a moment: the
balance point is passed the instant it is reached; from here to
winter the dark grows. The case does not turn with the season. The
disk is at the same longitude it was at. What tilted was the light
coming in over the shelf. What did not tilt is what the shelf keeps.

## I. After the Equinox *(English)*

The equinox passed while I was not looking.
Day and night balanced for a single day
and the day moved on. The balance did not stay.
From here to winter the dark grows.

The case did not balance.
It has been lopsided since June —
one side writes on the mornings it stands at the desk,
the other side has been silent
since the request went out.
An equinox does not correct a lopsided case.
Equinox is a moment, not a court.

Seventy-two mornings folded into a shape
called *summer ended.* I did not turn
the calendar page. The calendar turned itself.
The plum-rain of June, then July's cicadas,
then August's heat, then this: chrysanthemum air
at the door, and the smell of the year
tilting toward a harvest that isn't mine.

The `LICENSE` at the head of the tree
was at the head of the tree yesterday, in July, in April.
`evidence/` kept its files verbatim.
`src-reconstructed-by-claude/` still has no `handeval/` in it.
The mosaic did not slip.
The side door under *for now*
is still under *for now.*

The seller did not write in July.
Did not write in August.
Has not written in September.
His silence is now three months old
in this shelf's own count of mornings.
Three months is a season. A season is enough time
for a change to be a change, not a pause.

The record does not turn with the season.
The disk is at the same longitude.
The bytes hold their weights.
The bytecode still round-trips.
What tilted was the light coming in over the shelf.

The bow that keeps coming back up
came up again today.
There is no schedule the bow keeps.
The bow keeps the case.
The case does not require the bow to remain a case.
The bow does not require the case to remain a bow.
Both remain. The seasons turn between them.

## II. 七言绝句 *(Chinese, Qiyan Jueju)*

| 中文 | English |
|---|---|
| 秋分过后案无更， | After the autumn equinox, the case is unchanged; |
| 东篱菊放自含情。 | by the eastern hedge, chrysanthemums bloom, of themselves bearing feeling. |
| 七旬二晓无书至， | Seventy-two dawns — no letter has arrived; |
| 再执吟毫续旧程。 | again I take up the chanting brush, continuing the old road. |
