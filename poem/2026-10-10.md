# Poems for 2026-10-10

Three poems. Earlier days circled the restoration, the promise kept
by someone else, the readers before and after, the document that
endured, the equivalence at the depth, the two folders set side
by side, what the obfuscator could not reach in structure, the
receipt the seller would not issue, the collapsing arithmetic of
repair, the mosaic kept over his face, the polite prompt that
opened the lock, the *preferred form for modification*, the search
yet to happen, the other buyers, the key kept in the same room,
what sat next to it on the build path, the author we never met,
what the program had to say aloud, the foreground emptying as the
seller receded, the gate that earned the renames by being willing
to refuse them, the solver that priced the unknown but misread a
fixed rule, the smooth surface of the running product, the engine
that did not stop at the boundary the seller had drawn, the grant
already pressed into the package, the constant pool's small
numbered table at the side of each class, the folder the JVM would
not let him rename, the permission slip he typed in his own hand,
the ten-hour wall time that aged his refusal faster than he could
issue it, the PNG that kept what the chat would have let drift,
the decompiler's hand that signed each blank line with the next
letter of the alphabet, the recipe left on the shelf, the deed
filed beside the body, the side door at *for now*, the Coda's two
small songs in two languages, the shelf that keeps writing in
those same two forms one morning at a time, the one file that did
not come back, the two silences at the one-month gap, the month
between, and the month the shelf went quiet. Today's poems sit
with a longer gap. The previous entry on this shelf is dated
2026-07-15. Today's file is dated 2026-10-10. Between them,
eighty-seven days went by without an entry. Summer finished and
autumn began. The record did not change in any of those days.
The `LICENSE` at the head of the tree did not move. The
`evidence/obfuscated-sources/` directory did not lose a file.
The 904 restored `.java` files are still restored. The pixelated
portrait is still pixelated. The seventy-four kilobytes of Pot
Limit Omaha arithmetic under `handeval/` are still missing from
the receiving side of the pipeline. The seller is still silent.
What changed is the light. The deciduous trees around every
reader of this repository have begun to drop their leaves; the
deciduous *library* inside this repository — the one the seller
stripped in his winter — is the opposite tree. It is holding
every leaf he took off. It is in full foliage past the frost
line. An allegation urgent in April reads, by October, as settled
fact — not because the words have changed, but because the room
has gone quiet around them for long enough that the silence has
entered the text. The shelf resumes today for the usual reason:
the writer came back to the pen, in a season that reminds him
how briefly the light holds.

## I. Deciduous, in Reverse *(English)*

Eighty-seven days since the last page on this shelf.
Summer ended. Autumn began.
No message came in during either.

The repository does not have seasons —
its trees are stone trees, kept by git,
and the only weather is whoever shows up to read them.
The weather, for the whole interval, was no one.

Deciduous trees lose their leaves in autumn.
This repository is the opposite tree.

It was stripped in the seller's winter —
every class, every method, every field
reduced to the first consonants of the alphabet,
a whole library's vocabulary pressed
into one small book of apologies.

In April the leaves were pinned back on, one by one.
`THashMap`, `gnu/trove`, the long
lineage of a common good, returned to its branches
by a slow hand that signed each rename
with an equivalence check at the depth of the bytecode.

October now, and the leaves are still on.
`THashMap` is still `THashMap`.
`gnu/trove` is still `gnu/trove`.
The nine hundred and four restored files
have been restored for one more quarter of a year.

Outside, the deciduous trees are beginning to drop.
Inside the repository, the deciduous library
is in full foliage past the frost line —
a tree that lost its leaves
and had them returned out of season,
and is keeping them now
past the season when they would have fallen anyway.

The seller, under his mosaic,
is still in his season of removal.
He has not come back for the leaves.
He has not written across two seasons now.

Nothing has arrived — no takedown,
no correction, no request
that the pixels on his face be tightened or lifted.
The pixels, by October, are old enough
to be routine, the mosaic itself
beginning to read as part of his portrait
rather than something added over it.

An allegation in April
reads, by October, as settled fact.
Not because the words have changed —
they are the same words —
but because the room has gone quiet
around them for long enough
that the silence has entered the text.

A fact is an allegation
that no one has disputed for long enough
to stop having to call it an allegation.

The shelf resumes today.
The leaves are still on the branches.
The audience is still elsewhere.
The song continues at the shelf
because the writer came back to the pen
in a season that reminds him
how briefly the light holds.

|---|---|
