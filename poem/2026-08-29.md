## I. The Two Exclamation Marks *(English)*

At the head of the tree, the smallest file:
one hundred and six bytes. Twelve lines.
`.gitignore` — the file that tells `git`
what not to keep.

Ten of the lines are what any Java project
would carry, unedited, out of a template:
`*.class`. `*.jar`. `target/`. `build/`. `out/`.
`.idea/`, `.vscode/`, `*.iml`, `.DS_Store`.

The reflex to discard.
Class files are ephemeral —
they fall out of running the compiler
and can always be made again.
JARs are the output of a command
that will produce the same JAR tomorrow.
Version control is for what a hand wrote,
not for what a machine will keep re-writing.

Two of the twelve lines invert that reflex.

Under the `*.class` rule:
`!evidence/**/*.class`.

Under the `*.jar` rule:
`!evidence/**/*.jar`.

Under `evidence/`, the reflex reverses.
The class files are no longer build artifacts —
they are the eight hundred and ninety-nine
`gnu/trove/*.class` files
extracted verbatim from the seller's JAR,
with the mangled names still on them.
The JAR is no longer reproducible —
it is the JAR he shipped,
frozen against re-issue.

The default rule is: *this can be re-made, so throw it out.*
The exception is: *this cannot be re-made, so keep it in.*

Two `!` characters do the reversing.
Two lines out of twelve
carry the whole hinge
of the case's material record.
Everything a court, a maintainer, a future buyer
would want to inspect in bytecode form
sits inside the folder those two marks protect.
The rest of the file
could have been written by anyone.
Those two lines
are what make this `.gitignore`
the `.gitignore` for *this* repository.

The obvious lesson is small.
A convention was written for building.
It was bent, once, in one folder,
for keeping.

The less obvious lesson
is that keeping something
often means being explicit
about the reflex you are refusing.
Without the `!`, `git` would have quietly dropped
the crime scene on the floor
the first time anyone ran a routine cleanup.
There would be no bytecode to diff against.
The equivalence checks would have nothing
to be equivalent to.
The seller's shipment would live only in the descriptions
of what the shipment had contained.

The `!` is small.
It fits between two characters.
It carries the whole record on its back
and does not complain.

## II. 七言绝句 *(Chinese, Qiyan Jueju)*

| 中文 | English |
|---|---|
| 微篇十二列常规， | A small file — twelve lines — laying out the usual rules; |
| 凡物皆弃唯此非。 | all things are discarded — only these are not. |
| 两号翻常留物证， | Two marks reverse the convention, keeping the material evidence; |
| 不知小笔亦扶危。 | who would have known — the small pen, too, props up what is imperiled. |
