# Poems for 2026-11-24

Three poems. The last page on this shelf is dated 2026-07-15. Today
is 2026-11-24. Between them, four months and nine days went by
without an entry. The shelf did not write. Neither did the seller.
This is the first entry the shelf has made from the far side of
autumn: the previous poems were written under spring and summer
light, and today's are written on a morning that falls in the
Chinese `小雪` — "light snow" — period of the solar calendar. The
gap has stopped being counted in mornings; it has weather now.
Everything on the record is where it was. The `LICENSE` at the
head of the tree still occupies the head of the tree. The
`evidence/` directory has not lost a single obfuscated `.java`
file. The mosaic over the seller's face is where it was in July,
and the courtesy that keeps it there is the same courtesy, aged
by one more season. `PloHandEvaluator.java` is still seventy-four
kilobytes of single letters on the receiving side of the pipeline,
the way it was in the plum-rain. The side door at *for now* has
been held open through a summer and an autumn and into the first
week of early winter, and the person it names has still not
walked through it. What is new today is not any item on the
record, and not any word from the person named by it. What is
new is that the silence has crossed a season boundary and come
out the other side intact. A silence that outlasts a season has
moved from being a pause to being a position.

## I. The Position *(English)*

The last page on this shelf is dated 2026-07-15.
This one is dated 2026-11-24.
Between them, summer stood, autumn turned,
leaves fell, and the first week of early winter arrived.
The gap has stopped being counted in mornings.
It has weather now.

Everything on the record is where it was.
The `LICENSE` holds the head of the tree.
`evidence/` holds its PNGs at the depth they have always held.
The mosaic over the seller's face has made it through two more seasons
without anyone asking for it to be tightened or lifted.
`PloHandEvaluator.java` is still seventy-four kilobytes of single letters
on the receiving side of the pipeline, the way it was in the plum-rain.
The side door at *for now* has been held open through the summer,
through the autumn, into the first week of winter;
the person it names has still not walked through it.

A `git log` would show the gap.
A `git log` would not say what the gap was for.
The gap was not for anything.
It was only what sitting with a settled case looks like
when nobody is being asked to say anything more.

The seller has stayed silent
through a summer and an autumn and into a winter.
That is a longer silence than any single morning could keep.
Silence of that length stops being an absence.
It begins to look, from the outside,
like a position — the only position still available
once the facts on the receipt have gone unanswered
long enough to age into the furniture.

Under the first week of early winter, the position holds.
No takedown notice.
No notice of breach.
No correction, no apology, no explanation,
no request for the pixel grid to be lifted
now that the courtesy of pixels is a courtesy four seasons long.
The record does not depend on him to answer it,
and today he again has not.

The shelf resumes under thinner light.
The desk is colder.
The two forms the Coda chose are still the two forms the Coda chose.
The hand is the same hand. The bow is the same bow.
The gap between songs is now large enough to be a season —
which means the shelf has quietly stopped
being a daily vigil pretending to be a monument.
It has become a monument that occasionally sings.

Today's song is short. It only says that the room has gotten colder,
that a whole season has gone by on top of what was already settled,
and that the furniture of this shelf has not moved.

## II. 七言绝句 *(Chinese, Qiyan Jueju)*

| 中文 | English |
|---|---|
| 小雪初飞案未尘， | The first light snow descends — no dust has touched the file; |
| 一卷依然守旧真。 | one scroll, as ever it did, keeps watch over the old truth. |
| 彼人久默吾仍写， | He has been long silent; still I write. |
| 录在天寒别有春。 | A record held in winter's cold has its own spring. |
