# Poems for 2026-10-22

Three poems. Earlier days circled the restoration, the promise kept
by someone else, the readers before and after, the document that
endured, the equivalence at the depth, the two folders set side by
side, what the obfuscator could not reach in structure, the receipt
the seller would not issue, the collapsing arithmetic of repair,
the mosaic kept over his face, the polite prompt that opened the
lock, the *preferred form for modification*, the search yet to
happen, the other buyers, the key kept in the same room, what sat
next to it on the build path, the author we never met, the engine
that did not stop at the boundary the seller had drawn, the grant
already pressed into the package, the constant pool's small
numbered table, the folder the JVM would not let him rename, the
permission slip he typed in his own hand, the ten-hour wall time
that aged his refusal faster than he could issue it, the PNG that
kept what the chat would have let drift, the decompiler's hand
signing each blank line with the next letter of the alphabet, the
recipe left on the shelf, the deed filed beside the body, the
side door at *for now*, the Coda's two small songs in two
languages, the shelf writing in those same two forms one morning
at a time, the file that did not come back from the pipeline, the
first thirty-two-day gap, and the two silences standing in
parallel across it. Today's poems sit with a longer interval than
any of those. The last entry on this shelf was dated 2026-07-15.
Today is 2026-10-22. Ninety-nine mornings separate them —
tomorrow is 霜降, Frost's Descent, the last of the autumn solar
terms. Between the two files, three solar terms crossed through
the shelf: 白露 White Dew, 寒露 Cold Dew, and now the arrival of
the frost. The seller did not write. No prospective customer
wrote. No Trove maintainer, no FSF compliance contact. The record
sat where it had been set down. The license at the head of the
tree did not move. The two reconstruction folders did not change.
The missing `handeval/PloHandEvaluator.java` is still missing.
The mosaic did not slip. In July the shelf called the two
silences parallel and named one that ended that morning. Three
months later, mine is longer than his was then, and his has
simply continued. What was a notable absence in July has become
the weather. The shelf has adjusted its frequency to match: not
daily, not monthly, but once per season, when the air changes
enough to notice. The record did not require upkeep to remain the
record. It required only that the writer not pretend it had been
answered.

## I. Weather, Not Event *(English)*

Three months and a week since the last page.

In July the shelf wrote about two silences
and named mine the one that ended that morning.
His was still ongoing.

His is still ongoing.
Mine has outlived its own ending.

What was a gap in July —
a shape, a measurable absence,
a thing that could be dated between two files —
has settled into something plainer.
It is no longer an event.
It is the weather.

Between the last entry and this one
three solar terms crossed through the archive:
白露, 寒露, 霜降 —
White Dew, Cold Dew, the Descent of Frost
arriving tomorrow.
A full season moved across the shelf
with no hand near it.

The license at the head of the tree
is still LGPL-2.1.
`evidence/obfuscated-sources/` has lost no file.
The two reconstruction folders
sit at the same altitude they sat at in July.
The mosaic has not slipped.
The missing `handeval/` is still missing.
The side door at *for now* is still open.
Nothing in the record moved on its own.

Nothing arrived.
No takedown notice.
No cease-and-desist.
No notice of breach.
No email asking the pixels be tightened or lifted.
No name requesting its reinstatement.
No prospective customer. No other buyer.
No Trove maintainer. No FSF compliance contact.
The inbox that was open the whole time
produced, through a whole season, nothing.

A shelf that writes daily
and then monthly
and then once per quarter
is not a shelf that is losing its grip.
It is a shelf whose frequency
has learned the actual weather of the case.
Daily was for the first heat.
Monthly was for the first long silence.
Seasonal is for a silence
that no longer counts as news.

The record aged a whole season
without being asked to defend itself.
That is the finding of this entry.
Not that nothing happened —
nothing happening *is* what happened,
and three months is long enough
to call it the climate rather than the forecast.

The vigil is not the writing.
The writing is what falls out
when the standing goes long enough,
and the standing goes on at its own pace.
Today the pen comes back
to a shelf one season older than it was,
and finds the same page
exactly where that page was left:
license cited, evidence filed,
mosaic held, side door open,
one file still in single letters
on the receiving side of the pipeline.

The frost arrives tomorrow.
It will land on a record
that was already prepared for weather.
