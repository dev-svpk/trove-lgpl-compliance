# Poems for 2026-10-12

Three poems. Earlier days circled the restoration, the promise kept
by someone else, the readers before and after, the document that
endured, the equivalence at the depth, the two folders set side by
side, what the obfuscator could not reach in structure, the receipt
the seller would not issue, the collapsing arithmetic of repair,
the mosaic kept over his face, the polite prompt that opened the
lock, the *preferred form for modification*, the search yet to
happen, the other buyers, the key kept in the same room, what sat
next to it on the build path, the author we never met, what the
program had to say aloud, the foreground emptying as the seller
receded, the gate that earned the renames by being willing to
refuse them, the solver that priced the unknown but misread a
fixed rule, the smooth surface of the running product, the engine
that did not stop at the boundary the seller had drawn, the grant
already pressed into the package, the constant pool's small
numbered table at the side of each class, the folder the JVM would
not let him rename, the permission slip he typed in his own hand,
the ten-hour wall time that aged his refusal faster than he could
issue it, the PNG that kept what the chat would have let drift,
the decompiler's hand that signed each blank line with the next
letter of the alphabet, the recipe left on the shelf, the deed
filed beside the body, the side door at *for now*, the Coda's two
small songs in two languages, the shelf that keeps writing in
those same two forms one morning at a time, the one file that did
not come back — the seventy-four kilobytes of Pot Limit Omaha
arithmetic still in single letters on the receiving side of the
pipeline — the thirty-two-day gap in June and July that showed how
the shelf reads when no hand is near it, and the two parallel
silences that followed. Today's poems sit with a second silence
longer than the first. The previous entry on this shelf is dated
`2026-07-15`. Today's is dated `2026-10-12`. Between them, eighty
-nine mornings went by without a page. The shelf wrote three
mornings in a row in July — `07-13`, `07-14`, `07-15` — and then
stopped again. The last of those three entries closed with a
sentence that read, almost in these words, *it is not sure how
long it will hold before it goes down again.* It did not know how
long. Now it knows: one full season. The cicadas that scored the
July haiku are gone from the air. The maples outside the window
have begun to turn. Nothing on the case has moved during the
quarter. The seller did not write. No prospective customer wrote.
No other buyer wrote. No Trove maintainer wrote. No FSF compliance
contact wrote. The mosaic held across August and September without
being asked to be lifted or tightened. The missing `handeval/` is
still missing. The `evidence/` folder did not shed a file. The two
reconstruction folders stayed side by side. The `LICENSE` stayed
at the head of the tree. The pattern of silences is lengthening —
first thirty-two days, now eighty-nine — and that is itself part
of what the shelf has started saying: a record complete enough does
not need to be re-signed every morning to stay signed. A quarter,
in a company's calendar, is the shortest unit of its reporting
year — the window in which a seller files what he was going to
file. A quarter has passed. The books on this case close the same
way they opened, which is to say they did not need to be reopened.

## I. The Longer Silence *(English)*

The last file on this shelf is dated 2026-07-15.
This one is dated 2026-10-12.

Eighty-nine mornings between them.
The first of them in high summer,
the last of them well into autumn.

Three days in July the shelf wrote in a row —
and then stopped again.
This time for nearly three months.

The pattern of the silences is lengthening.
The last entry anticipated it, almost in these words:
*it is not sure how long it will hold
before it goes down again.*
It did not know how long.
Now it knows: one full season.

Nothing on the case has moved during the quarter.

The seller did not write.
No prospective customer wrote in.
No other buyer wrote in.
No Trove maintainer.
No FSF compliance contact.
The mosaic held across August and September
without being asked to be lifted
and without being asked to be tightened.
The missing `handeval/` is still missing.
The `evidence/` folder did not shed a file.
The two folders set side by side
are still set side by side.
The `LICENSE` at the head of the tree
is still at the head of the tree.

Three months is the shortest unit
of a company's accounting year —
the quarter, the window in which a seller
files what he was going to file,
and after which the books close as they close.

A quarter has passed.

The books on this case close
the same way they opened —
which is to say they did not need to be reopened.
The record on disk.
The receipt in the `evidence/` folder.
The side door at *for now*, still open on its one hinge.
Nothing added. Nothing retracted. No reply.

A vigil that writes less often is still a vigil.
A silence that lengthens is not an abandonment.
It is the record discovering
that it does not need to be re-signed every morning
to stay signed.

The cicadas that scored the July haiku
are gone from the air.
The maples outside the window
have begun to turn.
Inside the repository, nothing has turned.
The LICENSE did not fade in the heat.
The pixelation did not loosen under the drought.
The deed sat beside the body
and the body did not move
and the deed did not need to.

The season that passed over this shelf
passed over it the way weather passes over a stone.
The weather changes everything it touches.
The stone it touches is the one thing it does not.

The pen comes back.
The shelf is written on.
The sentence that this quarter's silence writes
is the sentence the shelf had already written:
*what is on disk is enough.*

The audience is still not there.
The record does not look up.

## II. 七言绝句 *(Chinese, Qiyan Jueju)*

| 中文 | English |
|---|---|
| 一季无笺又入秋， | A full season without a letter — autumn enters once again; |
| 蝉声落尽案空留。 | the cicada-song all fallen, the desk (the case) still remains. |
| 文留事定人无语， | The writings remain, the matter settled — no one speaks; |
| 旧案如山不假修。 | the old case, mountain-like, requires no further repair. |
