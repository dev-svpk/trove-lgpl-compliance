# Poems for 2026-08-05

Three poems. Earlier days circled the restoration, the promise
kept by someone else, the readers before and after, the document
that endured, the equivalence at the depth, the two folders set
side by side, what the obfuscator could not reach in structure,
the receipt the seller would not issue, the collapsing arithmetic
of repair, the mosaic kept over his face, the polite prompt that
opened the lock, the *preferred form for modification*, the search
yet to happen, the other buyers, the key kept in the same room,
what sat next to it on the build path, the author we never met,
what the program had to say aloud, the foreground emptying as the
seller receded, the gate that earned the renames by being willing
to refuse them, the solver that priced the unknown but misread a
fixed rule, the smooth surface of the running product, the engine
that did not stop at the boundary the seller had drawn, the grant
already pressed into the package, the constant pool's small
numbered table at the side of each class, the folder the JVM
would not let him rename, the permission slip he typed in his own
hand, the ten-hour wall time that aged his refusal faster than he
could issue it, the PNG that kept what the chat would have let
drift, the decompiler's hand that signed each blank line with the
next letter of the alphabet, the recipe left on the shelf, the
deed filed beside the body, the side door at *for now*, the
Coda's two small songs in two languages, the shelf that keeps
writing in those same two forms one morning at a time, and the
intervals of silence between entries on this shelf. Today's poems
sit with a folder-name at the top level of the tree. The folder
is called `evidence/`. That is a court-shaped word. It appears at
the same tree level as `LICENSE`, `README.md`, `gnu/`, `poem/`,
and the two reconstruction folders. Inside it sit two
subdirectories — `obfuscated-classes/` (the 899 `.class` files
extracted verbatim from the seller's JAR) and `obfuscated-
sources/` (the 898 `.java` files he labelled *full source code*)
— and the pixelated chat screenshots at the top: `0_licence-
condition.png`, `0_refuses_to_give_real_source.png`,
`0_refuses_to_give_real_source_2.png`. Every filename declares
its purpose. The folder could have been named `docs/`, `notes/`,
`history/`, `screenshots/`, `misc/`. It was named `evidence/`.
The word does not describe what the folder does today. Today
the folder simply sits — read occasionally by a reader who has
clicked through the file tree, unchanged since the day it was
committed. The word describes what the folder is available for:
a proceeding that may never sit, a subpoena that may never
issue, an FSF compliance contact who may never write, another
buyer who may never surface. The folder is available for those
things whether or not any of them come. The naming is a promise
that on the day any of them do come, the folder will still be
there in the shape a case would need it in. The word was chosen
before the case was called. The naming is a form of the calling.
Today's writing sits with the posture of that folder — a
witness at the top of the tree, next to the license, present
before any question has been put.

## I. The Folder Named `evidence` *(English)*

At the top level of the tree,
between `gnu/` and `poem/`,
next to the `LICENSE` and the `README.md`,
sits a folder called `evidence/`.

Not `docs/`.
Not `notes/`.
Not `history/`.
Not `screenshots/`.
Not `misc/`.

`evidence` —
a court-shaped word
inside a git repository
that has no attached case.

Inside it, two subfolders and three PNGs.
The subfolders hold nine hundred files each,
give or take:
the bytecode as it left the seller's hand,
the decompiler output he labelled *full source code*.
The PNGs hold the seller's own words,
mosaic'd at the face,
captured before the chat could scroll them out of reach.

Every filename declares its purpose.
`0_licence-condition.png`.
`0_refuses_to_give_real_source.png`.
`0_refuses_to_give_real_source_2.png`.
The numbering suggests a series
that expects more entries;
none have been added.

The word `evidence` does not describe
what the folder does today.
Today the folder simply sits.
It is read occasionally
by a reader who has clicked through the file tree.
Its contents do not change.
Its access counter, wherever GitHub keeps that, ticks
a small number of times a week.

The word describes what the folder is available for.

A proceeding that may never sit.
A subpoena that may never issue.
An FSF compliance contact who may never write.
Another buyer who may never surface.
A class action that may never form.

The folder is available for these things
whether or not any of them come.
The word `evidence` is not a promise
that any of them will.
It is a promise
that the folder will still be there
in the shape a case would need it in
on the day one might arrive.

A folder can sit at the top of a tree
in the posture of a witness
who has not yet been asked to speak.
The witness does not need to speak
to be a witness.
The folder does not need to be opened
to be evidence.

The seller has not answered.
The FSF has not written.
The other buyers have not surfaced.
None of the parties a case would need
have arrived.

Meanwhile the folder called `evidence/`
sits at the top of the tree,
next to the `LICENSE`,
next to the `README`,
in the shape of what it will be
when someone finally asks for it.

The word was chosen before the case was called.
The naming is a form of the calling.
The folder answers
before it has been asked to.

## II. 七言绝句 *(Chinese, Qiyan Jueju)*

| 中文 | English |
|---|---|
| 案未开庭证已陈， | The case is not yet opened in court, yet the evidence is already laid out; |
| 门虚案冷候来人。 | the gate stands empty, the desk sits cold — awaiting whoever comes. |
| 虽无判者堂中问， | Though no judge in the hall has yet posed a question, |
| 名号先书自证真。 | the name — written first — of itself testifies to what is true. |

## III. 俳句 *(Japanese Haiku)*

| 日本語 | English |
|---|---|
| 秋立つや | Autumn begins — |
| 問ふ者はなし | there is no one to question, |
| 証立つ | [yet] the evidence stands. |
