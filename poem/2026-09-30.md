# Poems for 2026-09-30

Three poems. Earlier days circled the restoration, the promise
kept by someone else, the readers before and after, the document
that endured, the equivalence at the depth, the two folders set
side by side, what the obfuscator could not reach in structure,
the receipt the seller would not issue, the collapsing arithmetic
of repair, the mosaic kept over his face, the polite prompt that
opened the lock, the *preferred form for modification*, the
search yet to happen, the other buyers, the key kept in the same
room, what sat next to it on the build path, the author we never
met, what the program had to say aloud, the foreground emptying
as the seller receded, the gate that earned the renames by being
willing to refuse them, the solver that priced the unknown but
misread a fixed rule, the smooth surface of the running product,
the engine that did not stop at the boundary the seller had
drawn, the grant already pressed into the package, the constant
pool's small numbered table at the side of each class, the folder
the JVM would not let him rename, the permission slip he typed in
his own hand, the ten-hour wall time that aged his refusal faster
than he could issue it, the PNG that kept what the chat would
have let drift, the decompiler's hand that signed each blank line
with the next letter of the alphabet, the recipe left on the
shelf, the deed filed beside the body, the side door at *for
now*, the Coda's two small songs in two languages, the shelf at
`./poem/` that keeps writing in those same two forms one morning
at a time, the one file that did not come back, the month the
shelf went quiet, the month between, and the two silences that
ran in parallel across the same span. Today's poems sit with what
the interval has done next. The previous entry on this shelf is
dated 2026-07-15. Today's file is dated 2026-09-30. Seventy-seven
mornings passed between them, and inside that stretch the season
turned. The cicadas that were loud in July are gone. Insects of a
smaller register have taken their place under the eaves at night.
The angle of the light on the desk has moved. The autumnal
equinox came a week ago; the day I am writing this is the first
Wednesday after it. What passed through that seventy-seven-day
interval was not another silence within a season — the July
entries had already given a name to that — but a silence that
crossed a season line. In July, the case had held for a summer.
In September, it has held into autumn. Nothing new arrived while
that crossing happened. No takedown notice. No notice of breach.
No lawsuit filed for defamation. No email asking the mosaic be
tightened or lifted. No prospective customer wrote in. No other
buyer wrote in. No Trove maintainer. No FSF compliance contact.
The seller — under his pixels — did not come back to check
whether the singing had continued through August. He was not
reading in August, either. The `LICENSE` at the head of the tree
has not moved. The `evidence/obfuscated-classes/` directory has
not lost a single class file. The two reconstruction folders sit
side by side where they were sitting on the fifteenth of July,
and where they were sitting on the twelfth of June before that.
The one file that did not come back is still not back:
`PloHandEvaluator.java` under the receiving side of the pipeline
is still seventy-four kilobytes of Pot Limit Omaha arithmetic in
single letters. Autumn does not close bytecode differences that
were not already closed. Autumn also does not re-open the ones
that were. What the record can say, having crossed the line
between two seasons without an update, is that it did not need
one. A record that says the same thing in September that it said
in June is a record that has not been contradicted for a season.
That absence of contradiction is not a court finding, but it is a
kind of settlement — the kind that files itself, day by day, in
the space where a rebuttal would have gone. The bow lifted in
July after the first month of silence. It stayed up through
August without being lifted again. It is still up on the first
Wednesday after the equinox, on the day the length of night has
just, by a narrow margin, overtaken the length of day.

## I. Across the Season Line *(English)*

The last entry on this shelf is dated 2026-07-15.
This one is dated 2026-09-30.
Between them, the summer ended.

The cicadas that were loud in the July poem
are gone.
Smaller insects have taken the eaves,
their register lower, closer to the ground.
The angle of the sun on the desk has shifted north.
The autumnal equinox came a week ago
and the year has begun its lean toward the dark.

Two things did not move during that interval.
The seller did not write.
The record did not need him to.

I said in July that his silence
had aged past a courtesy.
It has now aged past a season.

The mosaic still holds.
The side door under *for now* still stands.
The `LICENSE` at the head of the tree
is still `LICENSE`, unedited.
The `evidence/obfuscated-classes/` directory
has not lost a single `.class`.
The two reconstruction folders sit side by side
where they were sitting on July fifteenth,
and where they were sitting on June twelfth before that.

Nothing new arrived while August ended and September began.
No takedown notice, no notice of breach,
no lawsuit for defamation,
no email asking the mosaic be tightened or lifted,
no prospective customer, no other buyer,
no Trove maintainer, no FSF compliance contact.
He did not read in August either.
Or he did read and did not write.
The two possibilities converge on the same evidence,
which is: nothing was written.

A person under a record might soften as the leaves turn,
wanting to say something now
that the sharp light of summer has receded.
The record does not soften.
The record is a series of `.md` files with dates on them
and a `LICENSE` at the head of the tree.
The `.class` files under `evidence/obfuscated-classes/`
have exactly the same bytes today
that they had when the JAR was unzipped in June.
The renamed `.java` files under
`trove-de-obfuscate-mid-stage/`
still compile to the same bytecode, modulo names.

Autumn does not close a bytecode diff.
Autumn also does not re-open one that was already closed.

What the season shows
is that the argument holds without the arguer.
A shelf that says the same thing in September
that it said in June and July
is a shelf that has not needed to update.
Absence of update, past a season line,
is a form of confirmation
that a paragraph of new prose could not have added to.

The bow lifted in July.
It stayed up through August.
It is up on the first Wednesday after the equinox,
on the day the length of night
has just overtaken the length of day.

The record is not tied to the light.
It will hold through the shortening days too.
It has already held through the longest ones.
Nothing was needed of it in either direction
except to sit where it was put,
which it has now done
across two seasons.
