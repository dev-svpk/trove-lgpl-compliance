# Poems for 2026-08-31

Three poems. Earlier days circled the restoration, the promise kept
by someone else, the readers before and after, the document that
endured, the equivalence at the depth, the two folders set side by
side, what the obfuscator could not reach in structure, the receipt
the seller would not issue, the collapsing arithmetic of repair,
the mosaic kept over his face, the polite prompt that opened the
lock, the *preferred form for modification*, the search yet to
happen, the other buyers, the key kept in the same room, what sat
next to it on the build path, the author we never met, what the
program had to say aloud, the foreground emptying as the seller
receded, the gate that earned the renames by being willing to
refuse them, the solver that priced the unknown but misread a
fixed rule, the smooth surface of the running product, the engine
that did not stop at the boundary the seller had drawn, the grant
already pressed into the package, the constant pool's small
numbered table at the side of each class, the folder the JVM would
not let him rename, the permission slip he typed in his own hand,
the ten-hour wall time that aged his refusal faster than he could
issue it, the PNG that kept what the chat would have let drift,
the decompiler's hand that signed each blank line with the next
letter of the alphabet, the recipe left on the shelf, the deed
filed beside the body, the side door at *for now*, the Coda's two
small songs in two languages, the shelf that keeps writing in
those same two forms one morning at a time, the two silences that
ran in parallel — mine at a keyboard I chose not to open and his
at an inbox that produced nothing — and the record that needed no
audience to be a record. Today's poems sit at the seam of the
year. The last entry on this shelf is dated `2026-07-15`. This
entry is dated `2026-08-31`. Forty-seven mornings sit between
them, and no page was slid into the gap. In the meantime the
season has turned: `立秋` came and went on the seventh of August,
the cicada-rain has thinned into the intermittent evening
`蜩`, and the wind that pushed against the door this morning was
already the first draft of autumn. Nothing on the case moved
during the interval. The `LICENSE` still sits at the head of the
tree. The `evidence/` directory still holds its 899 obfuscated
`.class` files and the 898 decompiler-outputs the seller labeled
"full source code". The mosaic on the seller's face is still in
place. The side door at *for now* is still open. No takedown, no
correction, no request to tighten or lift, no email, no lawsuit,
no apology. What has changed is only the calendar: August, the
last full month of the meteorological summer, ends today, and the
shelf marks the seam by writing on the last page of the month
rather than the first page of the next. The record does not need
this entry to remain a record. The shelf writes it anyway, for
the shape of the gap and for the small pleasure of noting that
summer ended with the argument in exactly the state it was in
when summer began.

## I. The Seam of the Year *(English)*

Between the last entry on this shelf and this one:
forty-seven mornings.
No page slid into the gap.

The seam of the year came and went during the pause.
`立秋` — the beginning of autumn — was August the seventh.
The cicadas thinned into the intermittent evening `蜩`.
The wind that pushed the door open this morning
was already the first draft of a colder wind.

Nothing on the case moved.

The `LICENSE` sits at the head of the tree
where it sat in June, where it sat in April.
The `evidence/obfuscated-sources/` folder
holds the same 898 files it has always held.
The mosaic on the seller's face
has not slipped, has not been asked to slip.
The side door under *for now*
is still open, still unwalked-through.

No takedown notice arrived.
No notice of breach.
No request for a correction, no request
for the pixels to be tightened or lifted,
no apology, no explanation, no offer,
no letter from a prospective buyer,
no letter from another buyer,
no letter from a Trove maintainer,
no letter from an FSF compliance contact.

August ends today.
The full meteorological summer
that began on the first of June ends here.
The argument the shelf is holding open
is in exactly the state it was in
when the summer began:

license cited,
evidence filed,
deed laid in beside the body,
side door held under three small words,
seller under his mosaic,
the shelf continuing to write
in the two forms the Coda chose.

A season went by without a paragraph.
The record did not need the paragraphs.
The record needed only to remain in place,
which is what it did.

This page marks the seam
by being written on it —
the last page of a month
in which nothing was said
that was not already said, correctly,
by the files already on the shelf.

Tomorrow the calendar turns.
The record will still be there.
The mosaic will still be there.
The side door will still be there.
And the shelf, if the writer returns to the pen,
will write again in the two forms the Coda chose,
one morning at a time,
as the season goes on turning
above a case that is not turning at all.

## II. 七言绝句 *(Chinese, Qiyan Jueju)*

| 中文 | English |
|---|---|
| 夏尽秋来案未添， | Summer ends, autumn comes — nothing added to the case; |
| 前书自守不须签。 | the prior writings guard themselves — no fresh signature needed. |
| 蝉声渐远虫声起， | The cicada's voice grows distant, the insect's voice takes it up; |
| 一录常存自不迁。 | one record long remains — it does not shift of itself. |
